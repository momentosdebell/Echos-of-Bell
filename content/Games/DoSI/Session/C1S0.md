## 🎬 Recap
The winding road to Neverwinter proved to be anything but peaceful as two weary travelers—and two unexpected strangers—crossed paths on the dusty trail. Pushing forward through the countryside, [[Justin Case]] found his nerves frayed to the limit by the endless chatter of his companion, [[Edgar McGee]], whose rambling stories of dragon-geese did little to ease the tension of the open road. The quiet was shattered when [[Sappho Vindtop]] burst onto the scene out of nowhere, having spent twenty relentless minutes sprinting to catch up with the pair. Spotting an elder man and a handsome traveler walking together, she decided they would make far better company than her own lingering thoughts while traveling alone.

Though [[Justin Case]] scanned the surrounding area for ambushes, his paranoia reached a boiling point when the earth itself split open and an armored figure heaved himself out of a crack in the dirt. Defensive instincts taking hold, [[Justin Case]] brandished his carpenter's hammer at the newcomer, only to find himself face-to-face with [[Cerersio]], a weary goblin who had just clawed his way out of the Underdark after escaping the brutal slave pits of Menzoberranzan. As [[Sappho Vindtop]] marveled at the fugitive's weapon and quietly revealed a concealed flintlock pistol tucked beneath her clothes, [[Edgar McGee]] smoothed over the confrontation with another improbable tale, easing the tension just as the grand skyline of Neverwinter came into view on the horizon. With the city walls offering sanctuary from drow pursuers, the newly formed quartet agreed to stick together for a warm meal and a berth at the docks—unaware of what dark tides might be waiting for them at the harbor.

## 📅 Events
- The group conducted a Session Zero to establish player boundaries, campaign rules, and the initial meeting of the characters.
- [[Justin Case]] and [[Edgar McGee]], who were companions prior to the start of the campaign, were traveling together along the road toward [[Neverwinter]].
- [[Sappho Vindtop]] ran up out of nowhere to join them, seeking lively company over her own thoughts after traveling alone.
- [[Cerersio]] burst from a crack in the ground, startling the group and prompting [[Justin Case]] to draw his hammer in fear of an ambush.
- [[Cerersio]] revealed his backstory of escaping drow slavery and gladiatorial pits in [[Menzoberranzan]].
- [[Sappho Vindtop]] secretly showed [[Cerersio]] her concealed flintlock gun, offering him protection against pursuers.
- [[Edgar McGee]] entertained the group with his story about fighting a goose that might have been a dragon.
- The group arrived within sight of Neverwinter's skyline and agreed to find food together in the city.
- The companions discussed taking a boat out of Neverwinter's harbor, prompting [[Edgar McGee]] to recall his questionable boat-building past.
## 🎯 Quests
- **Reach [[Neverwinter]] Safely**: Enter the city gates to secure food, rest, and safety from potential Underdark pursuers.
- **Find Passage at the Docks**: Help [[Sappho Vindtop]] locate her scheduled boat at the Neverwinter harbor to meet her friends.
