## 🎬 Recap
Just when the party thought they were being stealthy, [[Edgar Mcgee]] finished his nap and rejoined the group and by casually defying all laws of gravity, sprinting full-speed along the cave wall where we were waiting. His majestic acrobatics were immediately ruined when he dislodged a huge rock, waking a terrifying fungal-tentacled squid we had so carefully avoided. A brutal brawl erupted where [[Cerersio]] narrowly dodged death, the beast was finally slain, and [[Edgar Mcgee]] was knocked stone-cold unconscious. 

Deep in the dark, [[Sappho Vindtop]] spotted a stirge nest, and [[Cerersio]] tried to destroy it, accidentally erupting a swarm of six bloodthirsty, flying beasts upon us. Mid-fight, a stirge latched onto [[Sappho Vindtop]] and began greedily draining her life, but [[Cerersio]] lunged in to save her, throwing his trident into a nearby threat. In one fluid, heroic motion, [[Sappho Vindtop]] snatched Cerecio's flying trident out of the air, sprinted back into the fray, and landed a legendary strike to kill another stirge, with so much force the Trident bounced back in to [[Cerersio]]s hands,  and he immediately hurling it one final time to spear the last remaining beast.

Reaching the Suncave to figure out what was killing the Myconids and their goo-covered leader [[Sinensa]], the group found walls of glowing purple and orange crystals. After everyone grabbed their share of precious purple gemstone chunks, Sappho realized the orange wall was trapping toxic volcanic fumes that were suffocating the Myconids. [[Edgar Mcgee]] didn't hesitate, he smashed through the orange crystal wall, letting sweet, fresh ocean air rush into the cave and saving the fungal creatures. But as the dust settled, the shattered opening didn't just bring fresh air... it also unleashed a roaring Fire Snake!
