## 🎬 Recap

The air grew thick with tension as the adventurers found themselves trapped in the oppressive, trap-laden corridors of the ancient complex. Pushed to the brink of exhaustion and battered by relentless magical hazards, [[Garth the Frank]] risked life and limb to press the glowing mechanisms on a massive sealed door, suffering painful force feedback with every failed attempt. Beyond, a grueling maze of force fields stood between the party and their goal. Undeterred, [[Garth the Frank]] dashed forward through the treacherous field to scout ahead, while [[Arvin]] channeled sacred magic to patch up his wounded companion.

Reunited at the inner sanctum's threshold, the heroes faced a dual-activation mechanism. Working in tandem, [[Garth the Frank]] and [[Arvin]] coordinated their movements to strike the final controls simultaneously. With a heavy thud, the massive stone door swung inward—smacking [[Arvin]] squarely in the forehead and sending him reeling backward! As the dust settled, the party stepped through the doorway into a vast, silent corridor, their weapons drawn and staff glowing bright. Flanking both walls were intricate, two-dimensional murals depicting strange humanoid figures holding glowing spheres of gold, bronze, violet, and green at various heights. Just as the party began decoding the secret sequence hidden within the painted glyphs, a ominous shift in the air signaled that they were not alone—leaving them standing on the threshold of a dangerous new puzzle with no way back.
