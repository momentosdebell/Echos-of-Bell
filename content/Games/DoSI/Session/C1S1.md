## 🎬 Recap
The session opened with a warm welcome and brief updates on external tabletop news before diving straight back into the adventuring action. The [[Games/DoSI/Party/index|DM]] set the stage for the party as they continued their perilous trek through [[Games/DoSI/index|Dragon of Stormwreck Isle]]. Having previously gathered in a local tavern and purchased a questionable tourist map of the area behind the tavern, the group had set off to investigate a mysterious, ancient grave mound marked by a prominent smiley face.

The party pressed forward into the dark, underground corridors, where mysterious red runes stretched along the walls. Before long, the group encountered a series of poorly constructed traps, leading to a quick realization that the dungeon was certainly not dwarf-built, but rather the haphazard creation of gnomes or gnomes' half-assed labor. Navigating through the unstable architecture required careful footing and clever thinking, though a few members of the party found festivities tumbling up and down the trap doors and pit hazards.

Despite the hazards, the adventurers managed to work together to overcome the treacherous terrain. [[Garth the Frank]] used his crowbar to pry a heavy door loose and secure it over an open pit trap, creating a sturdy makeshift bridge so the party could cross safely or make a quick retreat if needed. [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] led from the front, taking the brunt of the forward exploration while the rest of the group provided support and tactical cover.

Moving further inside, the party uncovered a hidden door concealed behind a plastered wall. Passing through the secret passageway, they entered a silent chamber containing a mysterious chest set against the wall. Upon closer inspection, however, the chest proved to be entirely empty, prompting the group to turn their attention toward the striking feature at the center of the room: a heavy stone statue of a monstrous, four-armed gargoyle-like beast.

Memorable banter echoed through the halls as the party admired the statue's striking, glittering necklace adorned with ten blue sapphires. While [[Arvin]] reflected on his character's looming life debts and tactical positioning, the party prepared for the worst. Before anyone could snatch the glittering prize, the stone construct suddenly stirred, its wings flapping as its stone skin softened into living flesh.

The session reached a tense climax just as the gargoyle awakened, forcing the party to brace for imminent battle. With the creature roaring to life in front of [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] and the rest of the group arrayed behind him, the [[Games/DoSI/Party/index|DM]] called for initiative rolls, leaving the party on the brink of an intense combat encounter.
