## 🎬 Recap

The party continued their exploration through the dungeon halls, grappling with previous injuries and tactical missteps. [[Garth the Frank]], despite having lost an arm in a previous encounter, remained surprisingly optimistic about their chances while [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] looked on, impressed by his companion's fortitude.

As they navigated the corridors, [[Arvin]] used magical manipulation to scout ahead and inspect glowing stones protruding from a stone archway. The stones emitted hues of yellow, blue, and orange, hinting at the riddles embedded within the labyrinth. Deciding to press forward, [[Arvin]] summoned a magical hand to test hidden mechanisms and inspect mysterious doorways while keeping a safe distance.

Upon discovering a hidden passage, [[Arvin]] relayed message communications back to the rest of the group. [[Garth the Frank]] and [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] quickly regrouped with him. Cautiously entering the new chamber, the party was suddenly ambushed by a volley of flying projectiles triggered by hidden room traps. [[Arvin]] managed to duck and dodge out of harm's way, but [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] took the brunt of the trap's attack.

While [[Thulgrim Goblinbane]] nursed his new injuries, [[Garth the Frank]] moved further into the room to retrieve a glowing staff lying on the floor and search the surrounding area for further threats or hidden treasures.
