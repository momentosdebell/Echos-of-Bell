## 📜Overview
**Menzoberranzan** (also known as the _City of Spiders_) is the most famous drow metropolis in _Dungeons & Dragons_ (Forgotten Realms). It is a dark, underground city located deep within the Underdark.

Key details about the city:
- **Government & Society:** Menzoberranzan is a theocratic matriarchy ruled by priestesses dedicated to the spider goddess **Lolth**. Males hold a lower societal status, with female Matron Mothers holding absolute power.
    
- **The Ruling Houses:** The city is governed by a council of the eight most powerful noble houses, typically led by **House Baenre**. Politics are defined by shadow wars, assassinations, and power struggles—the unwritten rule being that one house may destroy another as long as no witnesses are left alive.
    
- **Slavery & Gladiators:** The city relies heavily on slave labor, holding goblins, kobolds, orcs, and other races in captivity. Slaves are forced into hard labor, domestic service, or forced to fight in brutal gladiatorial pits for amusement.
    
- **The Academy (Tier Breche):** Young noble drow are trained at the city's elite academy, split into three branches: _Arach-Tinilith_ (for priestesses of Lolth), _Sorcere_ (for wizards), and _Melee-Magthere_ (for warriors).
    
- **Notable Connections:** The city is most famous from R.A. Salvatore's novels as the birthplace of the renegade drow **Drizzt Do'Urden**.
