## 📜 Overview
Neverwinter, also known as the _Jewel of the North_, is a major coastal city-state situated along the Sword Coast. Renowned for its resilience, the city earned its name due to its unusually warm climate—heated by elemental forces within the Neverwinter River—which ensures its harbor never freezes even during harsh winters. After suffering near-total destruction from the cataclysmic eruption of Mount Hotenow, the city has undergone massive rebuilding efforts under the leadership of Lord Dagult Neverember.
## 🎭NPCs
- Dagult Neverember: The Lord Protector of Neverwinter, leading the reconstruction and governance of the city.
- Seldra Tyrne: A prominent figure and agent involved in the city's political and trading affairs.
## 👥 Citizens
- **Neverwintans**: Resilient artisans, merchants, and laborers actively rebuilding the city's ruined districts.
- **Mintarn Mercenaries & City Guard**: Hired soldiers and local guards responsible for maintaining law and order throughout the Enclave.
- **Dockworkers & Seafarers**: Sailors, traders, and captains who manage the city's vital maritime commerce.
## 🗺️️ Geography
- **Neverwinter River**: A magically warmed river flowing directly through the city into the Sea of Swords, keeping the local climate mild.
- **Mount Hotenow**: An active volcano in the nearby Neverwinter Wood whose catastrophic eruption nearly destroyed the city.
- **Neverwinter Wood**: An ancient, dense forest east of the city, rich with fey magic, elemental power, and dangerous ruins.

---
#### 🍺Lodge
- **The Driftwood Tavern**: A well-known local establishment catering to travelers, merchants, and mercenaries.
- **The Beached Leviathan**: A famous harbor tavern built inside the refurbished hull of a stranded ship, popular among sailors.
#### 🏛️Landmarks
- **Castle Never**: The historic seat of Neverwinter's rulers, currently standing partially ruined following past cataclysms.
- **The Protector's Enclave**: The central, safest, and most fully restored district of the city, serving as the hub for trade and government.
- **The Hall of Justice**: A massive temple dedicated to Tyr, serving as a landmark of law and governance in the city center.
