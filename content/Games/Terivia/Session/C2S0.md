## 🎬 Recap
From Facebook comments to dnd group. This set of strangers came together from a post made by Alicia, and because we all lived close (though not so close that we can do it irl), both me and DM tagged along Alicia and her friend. 

Alicia and Sandra is fairly knew to dnd, and Bella needed a refresh on roll20 and DM trying out 5.5 rules for the first time. Basically we all went completely 100 on everything.

Surpriseingly DM manage to throw in a "small" encounter, with a giant burning skeleton and a puzzle. Just to test how everything felt and worked.
