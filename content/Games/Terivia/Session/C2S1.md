## 🎬 Recap
It all began on the open road. A group of random strangers, bound by little more than fate, found themselves sharing a bumpy ride in the back of [[Otto Riegelbaum]] the merchant’s potion cart. Pulled by his trusty horse Billy, the journey to the small town of [[Littlecost]] seemed peaceful enough, until bandits tried to ambush them. Luckily, [[Balsa]] and [[Zaria]] shut down the threat instantly, scaring the attackers off before weapons could even clash. Upon arriving in town, [[Otto Riegelbaum]] dropped the party off at the local inn, [[Harper's Hall]] and [[Merton Springkettle]], showed us gratitude for saving [[Otto Riegelbaum]], [[Merton Springkettle]] offered us rooms for free and we insisted to pay atleast half, ending up paying more than we should have.

We researched around town about all the trubbels we saw on the town board, starting at local [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]], where [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]] got quickly recognized by just the mention of her name, got useful information from [[Sebastian Otmund]], leading us to eventually the market.
After some chitchat we went back to [[Otto Riegelbaum]] for help where [[Balsa]] ended up laying down an 8-gold bet on [[Lilliana Telith]] to win the town’s annual merchant competition on [[The Scarecrow Awakening]] festival. 

Following rumors toward the local lumbermill [[Redmil]], the party trotted past a sleeping owlbear on the road, sparing it aa fight, and met up [[Gregory Durmond]] the owner. He delivered grim news: three workers had vanished three days ago. 
We started to investigat missing worker [[Frodrick]]’s house and it turned up full of signs of a struggle the local guardes missed, spilled booze, and heavy tracks dragged west, alongside eerie footprints that looked suspiciously like a giant chicken.

Tracing the marks into the wilderness, the trail led the party straight to the mouth of a dark cave, currently crawling with Giant Wolf Spiders. With missing workers to rescue, Blackeye’s bandits still on the loose, a stolen purple metallic box to recover, and even a missing cat named [[Lucy]] to find, the real adventure is only just getting started.
