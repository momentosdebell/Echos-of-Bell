## 🎬 Recap
It all began on the open road. A group of random strangers, bound by little more than fate, found themselves sharing a bumpy ride in the back of Otto the merchant’s potion cart. Pulled by his trusty horse Billy, the journey to the small town of Littlecost seemed peaceful enough, until bandits tried to ambush them. Luckily, [[Balsa]] and [[Zaria]] shut down the threat instantly, scaring the attackers off before weapons could even clash. Upon arriving in town, Otto dropped the party off at the local inn with Mertel the halfling, where they immediately set out to see what kind of trouble Littlecost had to offer.

[[Balsa]] laid down an 8-gold bet on Lilliana to win the town’s annual merchant competition. Business was booming, but real danger was brewing just outside the town limits.

Following rumors toward the local lumbermill, the party trotted past a sleeping owlbear on the road, sparing it a fight, and met up Gregory Durmond at Redmill. He delivered grim news: three workers had vanished three days ago. Investigating missing worker Frodrick’s house turned up clear signs of a struggle, spilled booze, and heavy tracks dragged west, alongside eerie footprints that looked suspiciously like a giant chicken.

Tracing the marks into the wilderness, the trail led the party straight to the mouth of a dark cave, currently crawling with Giant Wolf Spiders. With missing workers to rescue, Blackeye’s bandits still on the loose, a stolen purple metallic box to recover, and even a missing cat named Lucy to find, the real adventure is only just getting started.
