## 📜 Overview
The Scarecrow Awakening is a spring festival event in [[Littlecost]] celebrating the arrival of the new season and the protection of local crops. During the festivities, townsfolk stitch burlap and weave magic charms to animate straw scarecrow figures, bringing them to life to dance in the fields and safeguard the farms.
