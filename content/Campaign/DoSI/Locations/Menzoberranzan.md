## 📜 Overview
Menzoberranzan, also known as the _City of Spiders_, is a notorious drow metropolis located deep within the Underdark. It operates as a ruthless, theocratic matriarchy ruled by priestesses devoted to the spider goddess Lolth. Female Matron Mothers hold absolute power, while males occupy subordinate roles in society. The city thrives on political intrigue, shadow wars, and an extensive slave economy where captives—including goblins, orcs, and surface dwellers—are subjected to forced labor or pitted against each other in brutal gladiatorial arenas.
## 👥 Citizens
- **Drow Matron Mothers**: The ruthless female rulers of the noble houses who vie for Lolth's favor.    
- **Drow Priestesses & Noble Warriors**: The elite ruling class who enforce order and lead military houses.
- **Gladiators & Slaves**: Captive goblins, kobolds, orcs, and other surface races forced into servitude or bloodsports.
## 🗺️ Geography
- **The Underdark**: The vast, sunless subterranean network of caverns where the city is hidden.
- **Menzoberranzan Cavern**: An immense, arrow-shaped vault surrounded by towering stalagmites and glowing phosphorescent fungi.
    
---
#### 🍺Lodge
- **The Burning Goblin**: A infamous, rough tavern located in the city's lower districts where mercenary bands, slavers, and gladiatorial handlers gather.
#### 🏛️Landmarks
- **Tier Breche**: The city's elite academy complex perched on a high plateau, consisting of _Arach-Tinilith_ (for priestesses), _Sorcere_ (for wizards), and _Melee-Magthere_ (for martial warriors).
- **House Baenre**: The grand compound of the most powerful ruling noble house in the city.
- **The Fighting Pits**: The bloody subterranean arenas where slaves like [[Cerersio]] are forced to battle for drow entertainment.

## 📖 Lore
- **The Escape of Cerersio**: The goblin [[Cerersio]] was captured and enslaved here, forced to fight for his life in the city's brutal gladiatorial pits for drow amusement. He recently managed to break free and crawl through a deep subterranean fissure back up to the surface.
