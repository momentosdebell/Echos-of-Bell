## 🎬 Recap
 After some of us hesitantly sampled [[Edgar Mcgee]] rather average stew, we checked our supplies to see if we needed anything else. [[Sappho Vindtop]] bought a blanket suited for "three kobolds" (or more accurately, one human and a halfling) Just-in Case it got chilly. She was also flirting heavily with [[Justin Case]], exactly as it should be.
 
Once out on the trail, [[Cerersio]] leading the way, somehow managed to miss the completely obvious path of glowing mushrooms that was impossible to overlook. But after a bit of teamwork and a quick skirmish with some living plants, the journey went fairly smoothly, unless your name was [[Justin Case]], who kept complaining about getting "bitten by bugs." In reality, it was just [[Sappho Vindtop]] who couldn't resist sneaking a little pinch on his rear.

Reaching the cave entrance, we were met with deep water. Since a fair portion of our group isn't particularly tall, and given our lingering fear of the mushroom-like octopus creature we'd been warned about, we decided to take a short rest and wait for the tide to go out.

With no harm done and the cave entrance now open, a couple of highly hesitant myconids greeted us, claiming we weren't welcome. We insisted on entering and even offered them a peace gift: a bag of compost.

Once again, [[Justin Case]] got "bitten," but this time it wasn't [[Sappho Vindtop]], it was an attack by a weird necrotic mushroom intruder that the myconids didn't know. We stepped in, killed the fungus, and saved the day, which turned their attitude toward us significantly more positive. 

Even with [[Edgar Mcgee]] absent, there was still plenty of rambling going on, and someone, [[Justin Case]], claimed to be a doctor. Grateful for our help, the myconids insisted that we investigate what had made their leader sick and contaminated the rest of the colony.
