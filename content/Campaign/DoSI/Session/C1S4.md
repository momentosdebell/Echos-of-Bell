## 🎬 Recap
A terrifying giant fire snake lashed out with fiery wrath, missing most of its blows until a brutal strike smashed [[Cerersio]] straight into unconsciousness. Panicked, [[Sappho Vindtop]] rushed over and poured rum down his throat; shocked back to life, a furious [[Cerersio]] hurled his trident directly through the beast, slaying it on the spot. In the dust of the battle, an unwanted biology lesson to [[Justin Case]] on sex from [[Edgar Mcgee]] set an absurd tone.

Returning to [[Sinensa]] with the slain serpent, they found the once-dying goblin-myconid hybrid remarkably restored alongside her colony. [[Sinensa]] offered to transform them into myconid hybrids as a reward and we all kindly declined, but the group packed up and headed out, with [[Sappho Vindtop]] performing a _very_ thorough, hands-on spore check on [[Justin Case]] all the way back to [[Dragons Rest]].

After washing off the cave's lingering fungal dust and taking a long rest, the party awoke to [[Edgar Mcgee]] attempting to cook breakfast out of the raw, stubborn fire snake meat, no one wanted it. 
Guided by [[Sappho Vindtop]], they pushed forward toward the monastery, only to be greeted by the echoing crack of thunder and the discovery of ten slaughtered drow lying outside. 
They later reported their triumph over the myconid crisis to [[Elder Runnara]], only to overhear an outrageous scene at the statue of Bahamut: a goose named Charles McDuck II was actively calling out [[Edgar Mcgee]] for a duel.
