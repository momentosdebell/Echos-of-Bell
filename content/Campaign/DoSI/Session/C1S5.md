## 🎬 Recap
A simple reconnaissance mission to investigate why zombies kept crawling out of a sunken ship quickly spiraled into a personal nightmare. On the way to the coast, the party heard two kobolds screaming for help, only to realize the six undead chasing them weren't random monsters, they were [[Sappho Vindtop]] old crew.

The fight went from tragic to downright unhinged when [[Edgar Mcgee]] cut down one of the zombies and started playing with its severed head. Watching the remains of her found family be desecrated, [[Sappho Vindtop]] shattered and walked away from the battle, only for [[Edgar Mcgee]] to follow her and keep taunting her with her dead crewmates.

When the party finally caught up to her, Sappho was well past grief and burning with a quiet, terrifying fury. Brushing off every attempt to console her, she marched with the group to the wreck, where they discovered the massive skeleton of an adult dragon. Tapping into her Divine Sense, [[Sappho Vindtop]] felt the unmistakable presence of more undead waiting inside, and kept her mouth completely shut, letting the rest of the party board the ship totally blind hopeing for [[Edgar Mcgee]] to meet the same fait as her own crew did. When [[Edgar Mcgee]] satyed back on their rowboat she felt an bit of relif and of a bit of malicious joy just hopeing for him to die.
