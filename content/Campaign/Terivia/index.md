##  📖 Overview 
This whole world and campaign is a homebrew made by our DM [[Campaign/Terivia/Party/index|Johnny Larsson]], everything in this folder and setting is his creation and imagination.

Terivia is a world shaped as a private garden by the deity Terivian. When Terivian was murdered by another god, her essence was reincarnated into the world as the four elements. 

Today, she is revered by druids and respected by shamans who seek to master natural forces. The world is vast, divided between the ruins of a magical golden age and sprawling, tense nations on the brink of change.
Regions & Factions

-  [[Arnin]]: Game of Thrones (Beyond the Wall) meets ancient ruin exploration.
- [[New Vallon]]: Ancient Greece with a modern, corrupt twist.
- [[Kingdom of Forlorn]]: Classic high fantasy (Minas Tirith) on the edge of civil war.
- [[Lunmerian Empire]]: A strict, expansionist elven military power.
- [[Principality of Dugmere]]: Steampunk industrial innovation in a freezing frontier.
- [[Grand Duchy of Pridestone]]: Hogwarts meets Magic: The Gathering guilds.
- [[Unified Realms of Sirene]]: Feudal, samurai-inspired clan confederation.
- [[Wildlands (Kroshna)]]: World of Warcraft Horde, primitives bound by strength.
