## 🎬 Recap
Fleeing the caves with danger at their heels, the party made camp deep in the forest, where a half-dead [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]] still managed to whip up legendary pork and egg burgers for the crew. On watch, [[Zaria]] shared glimpses of her criminal past and late brother while [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]] expertly dodged every question, right up until [[Balsa]] jolted awake from a terrifying nightmare of [[Littlecost]] burning to the ground.

Morning brought weirder twists on an old battlefield: [[Zaria]] pulled a magical sword from the dirt that literally sings when swung, while [[Balsa]] realized her magic had vanished, thanks to the cursed staff [[Tor-Ulv]] picked up in the caves. After collecting 500 gold from [[Gregory Durmond]] for news on [[Frodrick]], the group hit the road back to [[Littlecost]] to bait the bandits, crossing paths with goliath hunters who looked eerily like the people who once took [[Tor-Ulv]]. The group finally arrived to find [[Littlecost]] buzzing with [[The Scarecrow Awakening]] festival, blissfully unaware of the fiery doom [[Balsa]] saw coming.
