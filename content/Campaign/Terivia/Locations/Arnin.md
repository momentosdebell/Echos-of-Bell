## 📜 Overview
Arnin is an ancient, desolate, and forbidden region that once served as the capital of the world. Following the collapse of its civilization, it now holds the ruins of ancient magic, legendary fallen flying cities, and mysterious monsters. The area is so treacherous and barren that it is rarely visited, guarded by two outposts to protect the rest of the world. Its dark and punishing atmosphere is reminiscent of "Beyond the Wall" from Game of Thrones.
