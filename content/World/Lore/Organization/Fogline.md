## 📜 Overview
Fogline is a pragmatic alliance of sea captains, dockworkers, harbor smugglers, and coastal pilots who control the waterways and sea routes. They specialize in moving people, goods, and sensitive cargo through thick fog, heavy blockades, and dangerous waters without leaving a paper trail.

While technically an illegal smuggling operation, Fogline operates on a strong code of honor. They are not pirates; they are sailors who believe that no king, empire, or navy has the right to block the free movement of folk and supplies.

## 🎯 Purpose
To keep trade, movement, and coastal routes open regardless of war, blockades, or political bans.

Their core principle: the ocean belongs to no one, and the fog covers all.

## 🧭 Structure
Fogline is structured around ships, harbors, and safe houses along the coast:

**The Iron Captains**  
A council of veteran ship captains who meet in neutral waters to divide trade routes, settle disputes, and set smuggling tariffs.

**Fog Pilots**  
Specialized navigators who know every hidden cove, sandbar, and secret riverbed along the coast. They can sail heavy ships through treacherous fog without light.

**Dock Hands & Lookouts**  
Informants and harbor workers embedded in every major port who signal when guards change shifts or when naval patrols set sail.

## 🏛 Operations
- Smuggling untaxed goods, weapons, and fugitives through naval blockades and guarded harbors
- Running emergency supply lines into besieged coastal cities or famine-stricken islands
- Utilizing alchemical smoke bombs and low-level weather magic to create artificial fog during escapes
- Maintaining hidden sea caves, underwater caches, and secluded coastal hideouts for cargo storage
- Bribing harbor masters, customs officers, and naval captains to look the other way

## 🌍 Reach
Fog Line dominates every major coastline, river delta, and port city.

- Secret coastal hideouts built into sea caves, cliffs, and abandoned lighthouses
- Strong presence in dockside taverns, shipyards, and fish markets across the realm
- Dozens of modified, fast-sailing vessels equipped with silent oars and dark sails

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Rat Ring]]:** Key partners. Once Fog Line gets goods past the harbor, the Rat Ring takes over, moving cargo through the sewers straight into city centers.
- **[[Darkane]]:** High tension. Fogline frequently smuggles hunted mages and magical artifacts out of cities, making them a prime target for Darkane ambushes.
- **[[Green Vein]]:** Open hostility. The radical nature-preservationists once sank several Fog Line ships over timber and fishing disputes, severing all ties between the two.

## 🏛️ History
Fog Line was formed during a brutal naval blockade imposed by a warlord who sought to starve a coastal province into submission. A dozen independent merchant captains and fishermen banded together, using their intimate knowledge of the local reefs and morning fogs to slip food into the city night after night.

After the war ended, the captains realized their shared network was far too valuable to dissolve. They formalized the alliance under the name Fog Line and expanded their reach across the entire continent's waterways.
