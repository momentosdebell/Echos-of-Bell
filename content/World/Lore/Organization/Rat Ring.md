## 📜 Overview
The Rat Ring is a massive, continent-wide underground network that controls the subterranean infrastructure, sewer systems, and secret passages beneath major cities. 

They are not warriors or grand schemers; they are the ultimate brokers of passage and information. If it moves under the street, the Rat Ring knows about it, cuts a deal for it, or charges a toll for it.

## 🎯 Purpose
To control all hidden movement beneath the civilized world and profit from absolute neutrality.

Their core philosophy is purely pragmatic: secrets are worth more than gold, and every lock has a price. They do not take sides in political or magical wars, they simply sell the keys to whoever can pay.

## 🧭 Structure
The Rat Ring functions as a decentralized, shadowy guild of scavengers and smugglers:

**The Sewer Kings & Queens**  
Regional bosses who rule the subterranean sectors of specific major cities from hidden, fortified strongholds in the deep tunnels.

**Tunnelsmiths**  
Engaged engineers and trappers who dig new passages, maintain sewer maps, and set deadly traps to protect their routes from intruders.

**Whiskers (Informants & Runners)**  
A vast network of street urchins, beggars, thieves, and outcast tunnel-dwellers who gather rumors, deliver messages, and act as guides.

## 🏛 Operations
- Navigating and guiding fugitives, smugglers, and mercenaries through hidden subterranean routes
- Selling access to royal escape tunnels, old catacombs, and forgotten city vaults
- Gathering, sorting, and peddling street-level intelligence and black-market rumors
- Maintenance and expansion of underground tunnel networks and secret trapdoors
- Smuggling contraband beneath city gates without triggering magical or physical guard checkpoints

## 🌍 Reach
The Rat Ring exists anywhere stone is laid over dirt.

- Total control of sewer systems, ancient catacombs, and drainage networks in every major city
- Hideouts embedded inside abandoned cisterns, old ruins, and city basements
- Widespread eyes and ears across slums, docks, and alleyways through their beggars' network

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Fogline]]:** Close partners. The Rat Ring routes Fog Line's smuggled harbor goods through the sewers directly into city centers for a clean cut.
- **[[Darkane]]:** Constant friction. Darkane tries to purge Rat Ring tunnels when mages use them to escape, forcing the Ring to set lethal traps for Huntsmen.
- **[[Slum Goblins]]:** Tense boundary wars. The Slum Goblins rule the gunk on the streets, while the Rat Ring rules the dirt beneath them, leading to frequent clashes over territory.

## 🏛️ History
The Rat Ring began centuries ago during a prolonged siege of a major capital, when a group of sewer workers and street urchins began moving food, messages, and escapees through the city's drainage pipes for coin.

When the siege ended, the workers realized they held more power and knowledge about the city's underbelly than the rulers above ever would. They expanded their reach, unified the tunnel gangs across regions, and built the continent's most resilient neutral network.
