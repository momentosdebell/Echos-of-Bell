## 📜 Overview
Darkane is a world-wide anti-magic syndicate and militant order dedicated to the absolute suppression, containment, and eradication of magic. They view spellcasters not as scholars or heroes, but as unstable hazards and walking disasters waiting to breach reality.

Operating in the shadows of major realms, Darkane acts as an unsanctioned, aggressive inquisitorial network. They track down, abduct, and torture magic users to "cure" them of their arcane connection or destroy their casting ability permanently.

## 🎯 Purpose
To purge the world of magic and protect non-magical humanity from arcane tyranny through absolute force.

Their core philosophy is simple: magic is an infection, and spellcasters are the hosts. Any act—kidnapping, torture, or execution—is justified if it prevents a magical disaster and keeps mortal hands in control of the world.

## 🧭 Structure
Darkane is structured into silent, cell-based units to prevent magical scrying, mind-reading, and infiltration:

**The Grand Vault**  
The hidden central command and deep-underground prison complex where high-profile mages are held, tortured, and studied.

**Iron Cells**  
Autonomous local groups operating in major cities. Each cell is led by a Warden who oversees local tracking, logistics, and abductions.

**Huntsmen**  
Specialized field agents equipped with anti-magic gear, alchemical suppressants, heavy crossbows, and cold-iron weaponry.

## 🏛 Operations
- Targeted abductions of spellcasters using anti-magic dead zones and alchemical traps
- Brutal interrogations, severing rituals, and alchemical torture to permanently destroy spellcasting focus
- Raids on black markets, arcane academies, and shrines to seize and melt down enchanted artifacts
- Infiltration of adventuring guilds, mercenary groups, and courts to identify hidden magic users
- Distribution of anti-magic alchemical poisons and suppression gear across their network

## 🌍 Reach
Darkane operates as a widespread, underground plague across the world.

- Deep underground facilities concealed beneath dead zones, abandoned mines, and city ruins where magic naturally fails
- Secretly supported or tolerated by anti-magic nobles, fearful rulers, and traumatized common folk
- Active hunting cells present in almost every major city and trade hub

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Silk Needle]]:** Open war in the shadows. Darkane actively hunts their spell-weaving assassins, while Silk Needle accepts discounted contracts on Darkane Wardens.
- **[[Sunday Market]]:** Darkane frequently raids their black market vaults to destroy magical wares, creating a constant blood feud with the syndicate.
- **[[Glass Eye]]:** An uneasy, toxic dynamic. Palace spies secretly feed Darkane info on rogue court mages when the crown needs someone eliminated without political fallout.

## 🏛️ History
Darkane was born in the wake of the cataclysm of [[The Great Storm of Valanor]], where an archmage's failed ritual erased a city and mutated thousands of innocents in seconds. A group of surviving captains, scholars, and bereaved commoners swore an oath that no spellcaster would ever hold the fate of mortals in their hands again.

What began as a desperate resistance movement slowly evolved into a cold, highly organized, and brutal inquisitorial machine spanning the entire continent.
