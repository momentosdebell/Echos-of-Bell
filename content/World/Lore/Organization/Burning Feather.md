## Overview 
House of the Burning Feather is an elite and proud bloodline of arcane scholars, high-ranking battlemages, and spell-smiths. Renowned for their mastery over flame-based transmutation and warding magic, their crest, a phoenix feather enveloped in blue-gold flame pressed into dark wax, symbolized absolute arcane authority.
