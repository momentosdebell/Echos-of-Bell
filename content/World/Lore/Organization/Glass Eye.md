## 📜 Overview
Glass Eye is an elite, highly sophisticated syndicate of palace spymasters, court scribes, blackmailers, and luxury information brokers. Operating behind the velvet curtains of high society, they view secret knowledge, political scandals, and diplomatic leverage as the ultimate currency.

While the common folk barely know they exist, the high nobility fears them above all else. Glass Eye trades in whispers, unseen observation, and precise political manipulation, ensuring that no secret in the realm stays hidden for long.

## 🎯 Purpose
To control power behind the scenes and profit from total information dominance over royalty, noble houses, and major institutions.

Their core philosophy: a secret revealed at the right time is more destructive than a thousand army banners.

## 🧭 Structure
Glass Eye is structured like an invisible lattice woven directly into noble courts and high government:

**The Mirror Council**  
A secretive inner ring of senior spymasters and master brokers who evaluate high-value intelligence, set market prices for secrets, and direct political interventions.

**Glass Lenses (Handlers)**  
High-ranking courtiers, diplomats, and major merchants who manage local networks of informants without exposing their true allegiance to the syndicate.

**Observers (Informants)**  
Embedded assets placed in high-security environments, servants, tailors, scribes, carriage drivers, guards, and court entertainers who observe everything while remaining invisible.

## 🏛 Operations
- Intercepting, decoding, and archiving private diplomatic letters, royal decrees, and noble correspondence
- Systematic blackmailing of politicians, judges, and military commanders to force favorable policy decisions
- Selling access keys, guard shift schedules, and secret palace routes to elite clients like [[Silk Needle]]
- Leaking targeted scandals to start or avert trade wars, political coups, and noble rivalries
- Brokerage of high-tier black market artifacts, forged title deeds, and suppressed historical records

## 🌍 Reach
Glass Eye operates anywhere high politics, immense wealth, and diplomacy exist.

- Complete dominance inside royal palaces, manor estates, high-court galas, and diplomatic embassies
- Hidden archives and vault sites concealed behind luxury textile houses, art galleries, and exclusive social clubs
- A deep network of eyes spanning every major capital city and wealthy trade hub across the continent

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Darkane]]:** An uneasy, toxic dynamic. Glass Eye spymasters secretly sell information on rogue or fugitive mages to Darkane when a noble or rival needs to be eliminated without political fallout.
- **[[Silk Needle]]:** Frequent business partners. Glass Eye regularly sells guard schedules, layout maps, and secret passage routes to Silk Needle's assassins.
- **[[Rat Ring]]:** Cautious tension. Glass Eye occasionally buys low-level street gossip from the Rat Ring, but despises their messy, unpredictable subterranean methods.

## 🏛️ History
Glass Eye was born during the "Decade of the Black Veil," a period of intense royal succession wars where half a dozen noble houses fought openly for the crown. Realizing that military force was far too expensive and public, a group of rival court scribes and advisors secretly pooled their intelligence network to sell information to the highest bidder.

By the end of the war, they had amassed enough leverage over every surviving noble house to make themselves untouchable. They abandoned their old allegiance to individual lords and formed Glass Eye to ensure that who ever sits on the throne, the syndicate always holds the true leash.
