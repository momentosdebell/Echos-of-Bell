## 📜 Overview
The 42nd is a rogue legion of veteran soldiers, officers, and heavy infantry who collectively mutinied against a corrupt military high command. Refusing to serve as meat for a noble's pointless war, they abandoned their posts, kept their weapons, and rebranded themselves as an outlawed "free army."

They operate as a disciplined, heavily armed mercenary syndicate and vigilante force. They protect frontier settlements, break aristocratic sieges, and target corrupt warlords, living by a strict military honor code rather than the laws of the crown.

## 🎯 Purpose
To protect the common folk from military tyranny, avenge betrayed soldiers, and dismantle corrupt noble regimes from the outside.

Their core philosophy: a soldier's oath is to the people, not to the crown that buys their blood.

## 🧭 Structure
The 42nd retains a crisp, lethal military chain of command:

**The Commander**  
The respected former general or high officer who led the original mutiny, overseeing high-level strategy and contract negotiations.

**The War Council**  
Captains of the legion's core units (Infantry, Vanguard, Engineers, Scouts) who plan tactical ops and manage logistics.

**Soldiers**  
Disciplined squads of veteran soldiers equipped with heavy plate armor, shields, and siege gear, functioning like a well-oiled machine.

## 🏛 Operations
- Conducting high-precision strikes against corrupt noble garrisons, supply convoys, and tax collectors
- Offering heavily discounted or free defense contracts to poor frontier towns threatened by bandits or warlords
- Raiding royal armories to seize weapons, armor, and rations for their ongoing war effort
- Liberating military prisons to recruit falsely accused deserters, political prisoners, and veteran fighters
- Training local peasant militias to defend their own villages against regional tyrants

## 🌍 Reach
The 42nd moves constantly across borderlands, war-torn regions, and outer provinces.

- Fortified encampments concealed in mountain passes, dense forests, and abandoned military keeps
- Strong sympathizer networks among commoners, rural villagers, and low-ranking guards in major cities
- Secret caches of heavy siege engines, weapons, and alchemical explosive supplies hidden across the continent

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Glass Eye]]:** Active enemies. Glass Eye constantly hunts the 42nd's command structure, selling their location to noble houses to prevent a full military rebellion.
- **[[Slum Goblins]]:** Vol atile skirmishes. The 42nd views the Goblins as predatory vermin and routinely clears them out of districts they are hired to protect.
- **[[Fogline]]:** Strategic partners. The 42nd relies on Fogline to secretly transport troops, heavy armor, and weapons across rivers and coastal routes without triggering naval alarms.

## 🏛️ History
The legion was originally the 42nd Royal Infantry, deployed to hold a desperate mountain pass during a bloody war. When their commanding noble intentionally withheld rations, reinforcements, and winter supplies to let them die for a political distraction, the unit's officers chose survival over blind obedience.

They turned their weapons on the corrupt command tent, marched out of the camp together, and swore never to hold a shield for a crown again. Since that night, "The 42nd" has marched under their own scarred banner.
