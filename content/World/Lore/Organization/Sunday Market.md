## 📜 Overview
The Sunday Market is a vast, nomadic black-market syndicate that operates completely outside the realm's laws, taxes, and trade tariffs. They are not a static location, but a mobile bazaar that appears in hidden warehouses, empty ruins, or shadowed docks, often for just a single night before vanishing without a trace.

If something is illegal, cursed, rare, or stolen, it is bought and sold at the Sunday Market. They maintain absolute neutrality, welcoming high nobles, dangerous cultists, and notorious criminals alike, provided everyone respects the Market’s law.

## 🎯 Purpose
To facilitate total, unrestricted trade of all goods, magical, physical, or secret, free from government taxes, bans, and moral oversight.

Their core philosophy: value is in the eye of the buyer, and gold has no conscience.

## 🧭 Structure
The Sunday Market relies on a strict, secretive organizational hierarchy to keep its chaotic bazaars orderly:

## 🏛 Operations
- Hosting pop-up black markets in revolving, secret locations protected by wards and illusion magic
- Fencing stolen artwork, crown jewels, forbidden magical relics, and untraceable poisons
- Providing neutral ground for rival factions, noble houses, and syndicates to make covert transactions
- Issuing "Trade Tokens" that act as an untraceable, high-value currency accepted across the underworld
- Smuggling exotic beasts, rare alchemical ingredients, and suppressed books across borders

## 🌍 Reach
The Sunday Market moves continuously across every major province and trade hub on the continent.

- Temporary setups in abandoned cathedrals, subterranean cisterns, ship holds, and secluded forest glades
- Deep connections with harbor masters, bribed guards, and corrupt officials in every major city
- Permanent safe vaults hidden in pocket dimensions or deep underground hideouts managed by the Market Masters

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Darkane]]:** Constant war. Darkane frequently raids suspected Market locations to seize and destroy magical artifacts, forcing the Market Masters to keep locations strictly secret.
- **[[Glass Eye]]:** Lucrative partnership. Glass Eye uses the Market to discreetly sell high-tier political secrets, forged deeds, and intercepted letters.
- **[[Fog Line]]:** Essential logistics. Fog Line acts as the primary maritime transport for exotic, illegal cargo being brought in to be sold at the Sunday Market.

## 🏛️ History
The Sunday Market began centuries ago during the "Great Tax Riots," when a greedy emperor banned all independent trade and levied crippling tariffs on basic goods. Merchant families began meeting in secret at midnight on Sundays, the one day guards rotated shifts, to trade freely in the dark.

Over time, as bans shifted from basic goods to magic and contraband, the tradition grew from a survival tactic into the continent's most powerful, untouchable black-market institution.
