## 📜 Overview
The Slum Goblins are a chaotic, territorial, and surprisingly resilient street-level gang network that dominates the poorest districts, alleyways, and shantytowns of major cities. While often dismissed by high society as mere vermin or mindless thugs, their sheer numbers, unpredictability, and ruthlessness make them an unavoidable reality of urban life.

They survive on chaos, crude intimidation, and raw opportunistic crime, controlling the streets through overwhelming wave tactics, improvised brutal weaponry, and total familiarity with the city's worst slums.

## 🎯 Purpose
To claim and hold territory in the gutter, extract every copper possible from the vulnerable, and survive against the oppressive forces above them.

Their core philosophy is simple: might makes right, the strong take from the weak, and the street belongs to whoever spills the most blood for it.

## 🧭 Structure
The Slum Goblins operate under a brutal, loosely organized hierarchy where rank is earned through violence and cunning:

**The Block Bosses**  
The biggest, meanest, or smartest gang leaders who control specific streets, market squares, or slum districts through raw intimidation.

**Gutter Enforcers**  
Brutish fighters and veteran thieves who manage small squads, collect protection money, and beat down rival gang members.

**Scrappers & Urchins**  
The bottom layer, thousands of young cutpurses, lookouts, scavengers, and pickpockets who swarm targets and act as the eyes and ears of the street.

## 🏛 Operations
- Protection rackets targeting local merchants, street vendors, and residents in the slums
- Smash-and-grab robberies, street muggings, and swarming lone travelers or wealthy merchants
- Stripping abandoned buildings, sewers, and border lands for scrap metal, wood, and contraband
- Staging noisy street riots, distractions, and arson fires for pay when larger syndicates need cover
- Street-level drug peddling, illegal gambling rings, and fence operations for stolen goods

## 🌍 Reach
The Slum Goblins exist anywhere poverty, overpopulation, and weak law enforcement collide.

- Total dominance across city slums, dockside alleyways, squatter camps, and ruined outer districts
- Entrenched hideouts inside abandoned tenements, collapsed warehouses, and makeshift wooden forts
- Constant presence in lower-class taverns, black-market alley stalls, and junk pits

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Rat Ring]]:** Tense boundary wars. The Slum Goblins rule the gunk on the streets, while the Rat Ring rules the tunnels beneath them, leading to frequent turf wars over basement entries and sewer grates.
- **[[Green Vein]]:** Mutual hatred. The Slum Goblins constantly clear-cut border woods for scrap timber and fuel, sparking bloody skirmishes with the radical druids.
- **[[Silk Needle]]:** Exploitative relationship. Silk Needle views them as disposable pawns, often paying them to start chaotic street riots to cover up high-profile assassinations.

## 🏛️ History
The Slum Goblins emerged from the utter neglect of city rulers who walled off ruined, overcrowded districts after plague and war. Left to starve in the filth, fragmented street gangs, war orphans, and desperate outcasts banded together under the strongest gang leaders to fight the city guards and each other for survival.

Over generations, what started as desperate survival mutated into a proud, violent gang culture that wears its gutter roots as a badge of honor.
