## 📜 Overview
The Green Vein is a radical, militant eco-syndicate and wilderness preservation network. They view civilization’s rapid expansion, logging, mining, arcane pollution, and city sprawl, as a direct, corrupting cancer on the natural balance of the continent.

Unlike peaceful druidic circles that attempt to mediate with rulers, the Green Vein uses sabotage, eco-terrorism, and guerrilla tactics to choke off supply lines and reclaim land. They are aggressive, unpredictable, and see any attempt to exploit nature as a declaration of war.

## 🎯 Purpose
To halt and reverse the expansion of cities into wild territories by any means necessary.

Their core philosophy: nature does not negotiate with the axe, and the forest always reclaims what is stolen.

## 🧭 Structure
The Green Vein operates as a decentralized, eco-guerrilla network divided into autonomous cells:

**Thornmasters**  
Veteran and wilderness commanders who lead local cells, plan sabotage operations, and oversee the creation of natural defenses.

**Vein Stalkers**  
Elite rangers, trackers, and beast-tamers who launch hit-and-run attacks on trade caravans, mining camps, and logging sites.

**Spore Weavers**  
Alchemists and herbalists who specialize in breeding toxic flora, aggressive fungal spores, and biological agents to contaminate urban supply lines.

## 🏛 Operations
- Sabotaging mining operations, burning logging camps, and destroying siege construction sites
- Releasing aggressive monsters, mutated flora, and fungal blights into urban border districts and trade roads
- Ambushing supply trains carrying timber, stone, and raw natural resources to major cities
- Utilizing secret woodland paths, overgrown ruins, and living plant magic to vanish into the wild without a trace
- Assassinating corrupt industrialists, noble landowners, and architects who order clear-cutting of ancient groves

## 🌍 Reach
The Green Vein operates anywhere civil engineering bleeds into untamed wilderness.

- Total dominance in deep ancient forests, mountain passes, and untouched river basins
- Hidden encampments built inside living trees, overgrown ruins, and subterranean root systems
- Strong guerrilla presence along frontier borders, logging camps, and trade roads connecting major cities

## ⚔️ Conflicts
- **[[Fogline]]:** Open hostility. The Green Vein previously sank several Fogline ships over timber trade and river pollution disputes, severing all ties between the two networks.
- **[[Darkane]]:** Volatile neutrality. While Darkane distrusts the Green Vein's nature magic, the Vein occasionally redirects wild magic beasts toward Darkane outposts as a distraction.
- **[[Slum Goblins]]:** Mutual hatred. The Slum Goblins constantly strip border forests for scrap wood and dirty fuel, sparking frequent bloody skirmishes along the city periphery.

## 🏛️ History
The Green Vein was formed in the aftermath of the "Iron Clearing," a massive industrial expansion in [[Ever Peak (Citadel Adbar)]] where noble houses clear-cut an ancient, sacred forest to fuel a decade-long war effort. When gentle protests and druidic pleas were met with steel and fire, a radical faction broke away from the traditional circles.

Renouncing passive mediation, they swore a blood oath to use nature’s harshest tools, poison, rot, and wild fury, to fight back. Over time, their desperate resistance grew into a organized, continent-spanning threat to urban expansion.
