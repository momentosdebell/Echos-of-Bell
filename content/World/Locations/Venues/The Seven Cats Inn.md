## 📜 Overview
The Seven Cats Inn is a tavern chain built on the idea that the road should never feel unsafe or unfamiliar. Every branch offers the same core promise: warm food, clean beds, and everyday luxury.

Despite being spread across multiple regions, each inn keeps has a recognizable identity in colors, service and hospitality, making it one of the most trusted stopover networks for merchants, messengers, and wandering workers.

## 🗺️ Location 
Found along key travel arteries.
Each branch is placed so travelers rarely go more than a day without one.

Several Seven Cats Inns are also connected through a **restricted teleportation circle network**, allowing verified members and premium guests to move between major branches instantly. The system is tightly controlled, expensive to access, and used mostly for urgent travel, high value cargo couriers, and trusted repeat guests under formal contracts.

## 🏛️ History
The Seven Cats Inn began as a network created by seven independent women who established a shared hospitality system across major trade routes. Their focus was simple but unusual for the time: safety, warmth, and consistency, without centralized control.

For many years, the chain remained loosely connected yet stable, expanding through reputation rather than ownership consolidation. Each branch operated with a degree of autonomy while adhering to a shared set of principles established by the founders.

As the network grew and became increasingly important to regional trade and long distance travel, it attracted the attention of larger merchant powers.

In later years, the entire chain was quietly acquired by a single investor, **[[Silas Kade]]**, a calculated and private merchant noble known for consolidating essential infrastructure rather than constructing it from the ground up.

Kade did not alter the outward identity of the inns. "The Seven Cats” name, aesthetic, and hospitality standards were preserved, as he understood that trust was the chain’s most valuable asset. Under his ownership, however, the system became highly standardized, with structured pricing tiers, strict accounting, and regulated expansion of the teleportation access network.

Publicly, he is rarely seen in any branch. His influence is expressed through consistency rather than presence.

Among staff and long term travelers, a quiet saying emerged after the transition:

> “The cats still welcome you. The hand that feeds them just changed.”

Ironically, live cats (animals) are no longer commonly allowed within the inns, as modern hygiene standards and network regulations discourage animals on premises.

Staff are not locally based. Instead, they are contracted through a central administration hub in [[Silvergrove City]] and assigned to branches as needed. Each day, employees use the inn’s teleportation network to travel from centralized city quarters, work their shifts on site, and return the same way.

This creates a rotating workforce system where training, service standards, and procedures remain highly uniform across all branches, while local staffing dependency is minimized.
