## 📜 Overview 
Perched atop a scenic, rolling hill overlooking the heart of the village sits The Shelf, a tavern that feels less like a business and more like a warm, living memory.  

Originally built over a century ago as a private home by the Moliner family, the house was lovingly sold to [[Bromhilda Winterfrost]] and late inherited by [[Agnes Ekblad]], who had already begun transforming the structure. Over the decades, [[Agnes Ekblad]] and her husband, [[Gustav Ekblad]], poured their hearts and hard work molding it into the legendary sanctuary it is today.

From the outside, The Shelf wears its history proudly. Intricately carved window frames, whimsical nooks, and overflowing flower boxes filled with vibrant blooms frame every window. The gentle elevation gives patrons on the balcony the feeling of floating just above the village rooftops, especially as dusk settles and the lights of the town begin to twinkle.

Step through the heavy oak front door, and the air immediately greets you with the rich, comforting scents of seasoned wood, simmering stew, and fresh mountain herbs.

## 🏛️ The Interior
At the heart of the main room stands a rustic open fireplace. Its flickering golden glow serves as the tavern’s anchor, on cold winter nights, travelers and locals alike crowd around its hearth to swap rumors, thaw frozen fingers, and share low, rumbling laughs.

Every corner of The Shelf is a living time capsule:
 * The Tables: Adorned with handmade, colorful crocheted tablecloths, each woven pattern tells its own quiet story of care and domestic warmth.
 * The Décor: Worn leather armchairs with soft, creaking frames sit beside antique mirrors that catch the dancing firelight. The walls are lined with hand-painted murals depicting the village's rich history, framed portraits of Agnes and Gustav throughout their younger years, and curious souvenirs collected from their far-off youth.
 * The Guestbooks: Leather-bound journals sit on small side tables, filled to the brim with heartfelt messages, doodles, and thank-yous from generations of regulars and passing wanderers.
 
 Whether it’s Gustav (affectionately known as "GusGus") picking up an instrument to play a soft, nostalgic melody by the fire, or Agnes greeting every guest by name with a playful spark in her eye, the tavern radiates pure hospitality.
 
 While Agnes and GusGus host the tavern six days a week for ten months of the year, the tavern stays open year-round. For two months annually, the couple packs their bags to hike, explore, or visit old friends from their adventuring days. During these months, the tavern's daily operations fall into the capable hands of their three 30-something apprentices, the Rosenkrans sisters, Pil and Rut.
The Shelf is more than just a place to buy an ale, it is the beating heart of the village, a sanctuary built on a lifetime of love, legendary adventures, and timeless comfort.
