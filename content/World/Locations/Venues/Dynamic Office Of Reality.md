## 📜 Overview
A high-end arcane shop based in [[Silvergrove City]], known across the continent for its execptunel well made magical items.
The shop exists physically in Silvergrove, but it is also partially “elsewhere”.
At irregular intervals, the same extravagant door appears in other cities, always identical in design. The problem is that there is a slight chans that you dosent exit where you entered. 

## 🌀Interior
You step inside and there is no inside, only a perfect sphere suspended in impossible stillness. The room has no floor, no ceiling, only a seamless curve of reality folding gently into itself like a thought that forgot it was imagined. Gravity does just stop existing. You remain weightless, not drifting, but _accepted_, as if the laws of the universe briefly checked your presence and decided not to argue.

Warm air moves like slow water, carrying scents of old parchment, polished metal, and faintly your favorit fragrant. Around you, shelves float in wide, silent orbits, bending geometry without breaking it. Books open and turn their own pages. Small arcane objects slide through invisible currents, occasionally blinking out of existence only to politely reappear somewhere more interesting. A teacup spins past you without spilling, because spilling would imply a surface that no longer exists.

At the center of the sphere sits a counter made of impossible craftsmanship, as if carved from bone, starlight, and law itself, anchored to a point that feels less like space and more like intention.
