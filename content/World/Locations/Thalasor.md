## 📜 Overview
**Thalasor** is the largest city in **[[Bram’vorn]]**, home to around 43,000 people across a hidden subterranean realm. Spread wide and fairly flat over stepped stone terraces on several rock levels, the city has only a few slender, rounded towers rising above the rest. Its buildings are smooth, round, and oval with a strange, alien-elven look in bright, clear colors, catching the light from the glowing crystal ceiling above during the twenty-hour day and ten-hour night.

Here, magic is woven into everyday life, lighting streets, moving heavy doors, and shaping daily routines, so having magic is normal, and having none at all is what stands out. Crowded, colorful, and full of life, **Thalasor** is a bustling meeting place of terraced streets, quiet corners, and ancient stories beneath the earth.
