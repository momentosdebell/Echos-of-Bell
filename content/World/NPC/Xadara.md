## 📖 Overview
Xadara is known by many names. The Dark Lord evil as one can be ruler of the Drenmar. Astrid lost his memories and started from scratch, learned how evil they been and searched for repent.

## 🕰️ Early Life
Xadara was born as a battle-hungry demon tightly bound to the demon goddess Zhriel, whose divine domain demands bloodshed and relentless warfare. While demongods rarely bow to anyone, Zhriel was ultimately subjugated and forced to obey Xadara, creating deep fractures in loyalty.

​Breaking away from the traditional hierarchy, Xadara sought out and became an apprentice to the entity who became the very first lich. It proved to be a fatal mistake for the mentor: Xadara absorbed their secrets, murdered both the master and rival liches, and completed the dark transformation into a lich. Afterward, Xadara began penning a brand-new treatise on how to achieve lichdom.

## 🕯️Shadows and Sanctuaries
Xadara's presence drew a persistent following of cultists and adventurous treasure-seekers alike. Arcane experiments flourished, including the construction of a machine designed to transform damage and a specialized sensory chamber.

Before leaving, Xadara hid a powerful magical sword deep within a burning inferno, accessible only through a mirror portal.

​Sometime after Xadara departed, worshippers of the Wanderer infiltrated Xadara's old chambers. They built a temple dedicated to the Dark Lord, complete with a specialized chamber housing cocoons, flesh-vessels allowing the Dark Lord to manifest physical forms whenever they chooses.

At some point, a severe loss of memory stripped away Xadara's dark certainty, scrambling the mind so completely that the entity genuinely believed its name was Astrid. Stripped of the past, Xadara was forced to experience existence from an entirely different, humbling angle, briefly seeing life through the fragile lens of mortality and innocence before the ancient shadows eventually began to creep back.

## 👨‍👧 Relationship
The Wanderer (The Dark Lord): A key ally in a tense, calculated pact, though built on shifting sands and hidden agendas.

Zhriel: The demon goddess of bloodlust. Zhriel despises the Wanderer, dismissing him as a liar, while being personally broken and subservient to Xadara.

## 🧠 Personality
Cold, calculating, and driven by an insatiable hunger for forbidden knowledge and arcane mastery. Xadara possesses zero hesitation when it comes to cutting down obstacles, or mentors, if it serves personal evolution. Retaining the fierce, aggressive spark of a demon combined with the cold eternity of a lich, Xadara views the world as a grand laboratory and a testing ground for power.
