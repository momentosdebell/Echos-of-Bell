> [!infobox]+ Stillflame Preparation Board
> **Category:** Wondrous Item 
> **Item Rarity:** Rare
> **Weight:** 3lbs
> ---
> **Properties:** The board produces a steady, controllable cooking heat without requiring fuel, flame, coal, or magical components. Pots, pans, and cookware placed upon its surface heat as though resting on a well-maintained stove. The temperature can be adjusted by touch, allowing anything from gentle simmering to vigorous boiling. The board remains cool around its edges, making it safe to handle and transport even while in use.
>  **Relic Trait:** This is unbreakable, automatically cleaned after every use, and functionally self-maintaining with no decay, wear, or loss of effectiveness over time.
