## Description
A quite pretty stone, dark, smooth, and cool to the touch. It fits perfectly in the palm of a hand and carries a faint, pulsing warmth when held.

## Properties
A simple utility charm embedded into a pebble, often used by miners, thieves, or prisoners who need light without the smoke or noise of a torch.

Glows with a bright light in a 10ft radius (and no dim light) whenever it touches bare skin. The moment contact is broken, the light instantly dies out.
