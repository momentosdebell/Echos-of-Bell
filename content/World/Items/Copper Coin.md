> *"When speech is forbidden, the cold metal speaks for us."*
## Description
A dark, heavy copper coin stamped with the exact same face on both sides. When held in a warm hand, the worn numbers along its rim faint red or icy blue, shifting and reshaping themselves silently.
## Properties
This coin is magically linked to a twin (or set) of identical coins. Created by imprisoned conspirators who needed to communicate without making a sound.
* **Silent Signal:** When the holder changes the numbers on the edge of their coin, the change immediately reflects on all paired coins, pulsing a subtle warmth to alert the bearer.
* **Coordination Tool:** Perfect for signaling "NOW", countdowns, cell numbers, or covert meeting spots across the corridor without drawing the attention of deafening horrors or guards.
* **Anti-Detection:** Because it relies on a subtle Transmutation effect rather than active vocal casting, it bypasses basic noise-based containment and low-level sensory wards.
