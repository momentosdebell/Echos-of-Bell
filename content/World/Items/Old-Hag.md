## ⚓ Ship Log
#### Day 98
Salt eats the paint and the mainmast creaks like an old crone’s bones. Ironneck thought she sounded too refined. The crew had a good laugh and took a vote around the rum barrel. As of tonight, she is officially the Old Hag. It suits her better, she refuses to sink no matter what the seas throw at her.

#### Day 213
Gideon handled the ledgers while Vesper took the wheel through a heavy northwesterly gale. She steers the brigantine as if she were born between two waves. Saltgrip and Nightingale squabble as siblings do, but no one on these waters holds a course truer than Nightingale when the wind turns.

#### Day 395
We picked up Milo's girl, Sap, a while back, claims to be a fisherman's daughter, but fights like a badger with a flare for black powder. When the Navy locked half our boarding party in the brig, Sap managed to hide in the bilge, covered in soot. One knocked-over lamp and an accidental powder trail later, the enemy warship blew into toothpicks. The navy's calling her "Sap the Black." We're just glad Doc didn't have to scrape her off the hull.

#### Day 730 
Sap has more sea-superstitions than an eighty-year-old boatswain. Today she threw two perfectly good jacket buttons into the sea because a seagull squawked three times near the mainmast, then spun in three circles at the cabin door muttering about Endrift. Pawnshop thinks she's nuts, but hey, we haven't hit a reef since she joined.

#### Day 942
Doc has officially stopped complaining about having to stitch up flesh wounds and cook stew in the same shift. He claims both require the same precise butcher's cut. No one questions his broth as long as he mends the men after a boarding clash. Whisp keeps an eye on the ledgers to ensure Doc doesn't mix the medical alcohol with the galley rum.

#### Day 1056 
Moody and Powder Keg loaded the cannons in thick fog. Sap was first over the bulwarks alongside Sharks and Pawnshop, firing pistol with one hand and swinging sword with the other. You can't tell if she's smiling because she loves the fight or because she's terrified of her own superstitions. We took three chests of silver and two casks of salt beef.

#### Day 1178
Dropped anchor near [[Rivergold (Maxal)]] to load up on fresh water and [[Sunday Market]] black powder. Sap went ashore to "say a quick hi" to her folks and pick up supplies from her cousin [[Rocksan Tickelfoot| Rocksan]]. We gave her a strict deadline before the tide turned. Local guards started snooping around the docks looking for pirates, so we had to cast off fast.

#### Day 1180 
Sap missed the boat. Literally. We waited as long as we dared, but with navy cutters patrolling the harbor, Ironneck had to give the order to weigh anchor. Saltgrip left a word with the local [[Fogline]] smugglers to keep an eye on her. She's a smart kid, she'll find a way back to us eventually.

#### Day 1302 
20 of us aboard now, missing our little black-powder powerhouse. We're cutting through the heavy squalls toward the jagged shores of Stormwreck Isle. Dangerous waters, ancient ruins, and heavy reefs ahead. If Sap is half as sharp as her superstitions suggest, she'll track the Old Hag down before we drop anchor.
