> [!infobox]+ Doublewake Rolling Pin
> **Category:** Tool / Utility Weapon
> **Item Rarity:** Rare
> **Weight:** 3lbs
> **Attack Type:** Melee
> **Range:** 5 ft.
> **Damage:** 1d4 
> **Damage Type:**  Bludgeoning
> ---
> **Properties:** When used in baking, creates a mild magical pressure field that increases dough expansion efficiency, improving yield without extra ingredients.
> In combat, it acts as a light blunt weapon with a delayed echo strike effect. On a successful hit, the impact repeats at the start of the target’s next turn, dealing an additional **1d4 bludgeoning damage to all creatures within 5 ft of the target**.
>  **Relic Trait:** This is unbreakable, automatically cleaned after every use, and functionally self-maintaining with no decay, wear, or loss of effectiveness over time.
