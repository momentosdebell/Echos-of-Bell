> [!infobox]+ Evercold Tankard
> **Category:** Wondrous Item 
> **Item Rarity:** Rare
> **Weight:** 0.5 lbs
> ---
> **Properties:** The tankard continuously replenishes itself with a cool, refreshing beverage appropriate to the drinker's tastes. The liquid is always clean, safe to consume, and served at an ideal drinking temperature. The tankard cannot produce alcoholic beverages stronger than ordinary ale.
