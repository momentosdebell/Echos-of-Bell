The title **The Uncrowned** is a royal designation granted by the crown in recognition of an unidentified woman who assisted the [[Queen Lilliana Yllaris Everfall]] in a fetal wond, struck by raiders during a seege.

It is the rarest royal honors in the kingdom, because it is not granted for service to the state.

It is granted for **existence at the exact moment survival depended on an unknown person acting without authority**.

---

## 📖 Overview

- **Title Type:** Royal honorary designation
- **Granted by:** The Crown 
- **Recipient:** Unknown woman ([[Molliebeth Bramblevick]])
- **Core Meaning:** “One who bore no crown, yet ensured the crown could exist”
- **Status:** Permanent ceremonial recognition

The identity of the woman was never formally confirmed in court records.

Only half a blanket were recorded as proof of this event.

---

## ⚔️ The Siege Event

The queen was traveling outside the capital under standard diplomatic and inspection duties when the incident occurred.

During the stay in a regional town:

- Armed forces surrounded the settlement
- Trade routes collapsed instantly
- Medical support from royal escort units became inaccessible
- Evacuation was no longer possible

The siege was not aimed at the royal party specifically.

But they became trapped inside it.

During this time, the queen got struck by a fatle wound.

With no access to court physicians or trained royal attendants, the situation became critical.

---
## 👑 Why “The Uncrowned”

The crown later formalized the title not as nobility, but as **a permanent acknowledgment of unrecorded intervention**.

The meaning:

- she held no authority
- she held no title
- she held no recognition during the event
- yet she preserved the life of the Queen 

Thus she became:

> The Uncrowned

Meaning:

A person without crown who ensured a crown would still exist.

---

## 📜 Royal Interpretation

Court records classify the event as:

- emergency civilian medical intervention during siege conditions
- unregistered assistance medical care
- critical survival support for royal lineage

Official royal inscription states:

> “The future of the crown was carried by one who never stood beneath it.”

---

## 🪶 Cultural Legacy

Over time, the title evolved beyond the single event:

- Some traditions treat it as a symbol for unseen lifesavers
- Some scholars believe the woman’s identity was intentionally never recorded for her safety
- The court still references “The Uncrowned Moment” during succession ceremonies
