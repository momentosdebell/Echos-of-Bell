The title **Keeper of the Winter Table** is an official honor granted by [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]] to Molliebeth Hamilton ([[Molliebeth Bramblevick]]) for demonstrate extraordinary achievement in sustaining populations through extreme winter scarcity.

It is not a rank of command.

It is a recognition of **impossible efficiency under collapse conditions**, where food supply should have failed, but did not.

[[Molliebeth Bramblevick]] received the title after the **[[Ambervale]] Winter Operation**, where famine conditions were reversed through unconventional ration extension, restructuring of food flow, and rapid field adaptation beyond standard Circle doctrine.

---

## 📜 Overview

- **Title Type:** High recognition humanitarian designation
- **Granted by:** [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]]
- **Recipient:** Then Molliebeth Hamilton (later [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]]) 
- **Awarded for:** Ambervale Winter Operation
- **Core Achievement:** Extreme food extension efficiency during famine collapse conditions
- **Status:** Active honorary title, field recognized

---
### ❄️ Keeper of the Winter Table

- She preserved life under winter collapse conditions
- She extended food systems beyond modeled capacity
- She stabilized a population through ration transformation rather than supply increase
- She demonstrated repeatable survival logic under scarcity extremes

The title was assigned as both **honor and documentation marker**, used to classify similar future field anomalies.

## ⚡ Notable Events

- Ambervale Winter Operation: prevention of projected famine collapse
- First documented large scale use of adaptive ration cycling in [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]] field history
- Recognition of non supply based survival extension methodology
- Integration of Ambervale case study into [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]] field logistics training
