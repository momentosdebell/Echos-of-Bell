Is the most sacred, rare, and prestigious gathering held by [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]] Occurring only once every one hundred years, this legendary centennial conclave brings together the realm’s finest field chefs, logistic masters, and hedge-wizards to compete in trials of culinary craft, resourcefulness, and miracles of sustenance.

Unlike traditional noble tournaments that celebrate blood sports or destructive magic, the Bloom is a celebration of preservation. It tests a competitor’s ability to conjure hope from ashes, stretch meager rations into life-saving feasts, and weave subtle magic into the very food meant to sustain collapsing populations.

The victor of the Bloom is awarded the Circle’s most guarded treasures: The Blooming Table Relics, magical artifacts capable of altering the tides of famine, purifying blighted lands, and ensuring that no community under the holder’s watch ever goes hungry.

## 📖 Overview

- **Hosting Organization:** [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]] (COBT) 
- **Frequency:** Once every 100 years 
- **Core Focus:** Crisis culinary arts, magical food stabilization, and field logistics under extreme pressure.
- **Primary Reward:** The Blooming Table Relics (legendary humanitarian artifacts).
- **Public Perception:** While the common folk view the event as a mythic celebration of mercy and survival, the nobility quietly watch the results, well aware that the master chef who walks away with the relics holds the power to stabilize or completely shift the logistical balance of the continent.
