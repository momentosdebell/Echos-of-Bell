## 🌱Natural Warforge 

#### Verdant Footsteps
Sequoia leaves no ordinary tracks behind. Wherever he walks, a small, vibrant trail of flora sprouts in his wake instead of footprints. The growth adapts instantly to his current environment: wild clover and moss might push through the cracks of city cobblestones, glowing fungi might sprout on damp cave floors, or elegant, manicured blossoms might emerge upon the polished hardwood of a noble’s estate.
#### Environmental Camouflage
As a creature of living wood and deep magic, Sequoia’s ability to blend into his surroundings is entirely dictated by nature's presence. The Dungeon Master has the final say on whether the current location can plausibly harbor roots or foliage.

**Natural Affinity** (+5 Stealth): He gains a +5 bonus to Stealth checks in environments where trees, dense vegetation, or ancient roots naturally belong, such as deep forests, overgrown ruins, or root-riddled caverns.

**Unnatural Presence** (-5 Stealth): Conversely, he suffers a -5 penalty to Stealth checks in sterile or enclosed spaces where a giant tree-man is jarringly out of place, such as a noble's pristine bedroom or a stone treasury.

#### Emotional Harvest
Sequoia naturally grows unique fruits from his canopy, the flavor of which is entirely tethered to his current emotional state. Those who partake can instantly taste his true feelings:

**Joy & Happiness:** The fruits become incredibly sweet, rich, and bursting with vibrant flavor.

**Grief & Sadness:** The flesh of the fruit turns intensely bitter, sharp, and hard to swallow.

**Anger & Stress:** The fruit might taste spicy, or leaving a burning sensation on the tongue.

#### Seasonal Metamorphosis
Sequoia’s physical form shifts dynamically to reflect the surrounding ambient temperature, acting as a living calendar:
![[Sequoia-Alt.png]]

**High Temperatures:** His canopy thickens with lush, vibrant green leaves, trailing vines, and a heavy abundance of fruits.

**Temperate Climates:** His foliage thins slightly, making way for an explosion of colorful flowers and blooming buds.

**Cooling Climates:** His leaves undergo a breathtaking transition, changing into brilliant shades of fiery orange, deep yellow, and rich red.

**Freezing Temperatures:** His leaves wither and fall away completely. His exposed wood becomes hardened and dormant, his skin functioning and looking exactly like an ordinary, wintering tree.

#### Innate Spell Alterations
All of Sequoia's magic flows directly from within his own biological and mechanical core. Rather than invoking external forces, his spells are physical, nature-based extensions of his living ironwood body.

**Flame Blade, now Thorn Fists**
Instead of conjuring a blazing blade of fire, Sequoia channels primal energy into his hands. His fists erupt with massive, jagged, rose-like thorns. When he strikes, these heavy thorns deliver brutal piercing trauma infused with a toxic sap, dealing Poison damage instead of the standard fire damage.

**Ice Knife now Resin Shard** 
Sequoia hardens his natural sap into a dense, crystallized projectile. He hurls a heavy clump of solid resin at a target, dealing Bludgeoning damage on impact instead of piercing. Immediately after impact, the volatile resin ball shatters and explodes violently, sending sharp wood-and-resin shrapnel flying outward to deal Piercing damage to all nearby targets instead of cold damage.

**Chill Touch now Venomous Root**
Instead of conjuring a ghostly hand, Sequoia unleashes a wine. It whips out directly from his wrist toward the target.
