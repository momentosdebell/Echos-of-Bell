## 📖 Overview

Talia Bramblevick, commonly known as **Sourdough Tally**, is a master baker and long-time resident of [[Ambervale]]. She is best known as the owner of **[[The Rolling Pin]]**, one of the town's oldest bakeries, and as the primary mentor figure in the life of [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]].

Though never formally holding a leadership position within [[The Circle of the Blooming Table]], Tally is widely respected for her previous humanitarian work, culinary expertise, and contributions to famine relief efforts throughout the region.

She is the confirmed owner of the Blooming Table Relic known as **[[Doublewake Rolling Pin]]**

---

## 🕰️ Early Life

Talia Bramblevick was born during a period of severe food shortages in the coastal settlement of Ambervale. Raised in a family of bakers, she developed an early understanding of food preservation, rationing, and community support.

Throughout her early adulthood, Tally traveled extensively, studying culinary traditions, grain storage methods, fermentation techniques, and food logistics from numerous cultures and regions.

Her experiences shaped a philosophy that would later define her life's work: food should be treated as a tool for survival before profit.

---

## 🤝 Humanitarian Work

During several regional famines, Tally became known for developing efficient field bakery systems capable of feeding large populations with limited resources.

Her work eventually brought her into contact with **The Circle of the Blooming Table**, a humanitarian organization specializing in famine relief, disaster response, and civilian support operations.

Although repeatedly offered positions of authority, Tally consistently declined formal leadership roles, choosing instead to remain directly involved in practical relief work.

Her reputation within the Circle remains exceptionally high, particularly among logistical and culinary specialists.

---

## 👨‍👧 Relationship with Mollibeth Bramblevick

Tallia Bramblevick is widely regarded as the most influential figure in [[Molliebeth Bramblevick]]'s life.

While House [[Hamilton]] provided her formal education and noble status, Talia taught many of the practical skills that later defined her career, including:

- Cooking
- Food preservation
- Crisis logistics
- Herb usage
- Community care
- Humanitarian field work
    

Their relationship is commonly described as resembling that of a sisters.

Following the deaths of Mollibeth's children and her separation from House Hamilton, Thalia remained one of her few consistent supporters.

---

## 🧠 Personality

Talia is generally described as patient, practical, and deeply compassionate.

She places significant value on service, community support, and personal responsibility. Despite her accomplishments, she actively avoids public recognition and prefers direct action over titles or prestige.

Among friends and associates, she is known for providing advice through simple observations rather than lengthy speeches.

---

## 🔒 Known Secrets

### The Capital Offer

Several decades ago, a major guild from the capital reportedly offered to purchase Talia's proprietary sourdough recipe, bakery, and surrounding property.

The offer would have provided enough wealth for permanent retirement.

Tally declined.

The exact details remain private, though some sources suggest her decision was influenced by a desire to preserve The Rolling Pin as a safe haven for members of the local community.
