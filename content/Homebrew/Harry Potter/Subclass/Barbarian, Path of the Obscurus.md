### **Level 3: Suppressed Magic** 
Your suppressed inner magic reacts volatilely to the weave. Whenever a spell is cast within 30 feet of you, your body crackles with dark energy, and your Walking Speed increases by 10 feet until the end of your next turn. This effect can only trigger once per round
### Level 3: Obscurus Unleashed
When you enter your Rage, your body and equipment dissolve into a churning cloud of dark energy. While your Rage is active, your melee weapon attacks deal Force damage instead of their normal damage type, and the reach of your melee weapon attacks increases by 5 feet. Additionally, at the start of each of your turns while your Rage is active, each creature within 5 feet of you takes Force damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
### Level 6: Untamed Flight
Your dark energy billows into a chaotic mass that lifts you off the ground. While your Rage is active, you gain a Fly speed equal to your Speed. While flying this way, you can move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing, and you do not provoke opportunity attacks from any creature whose space you pass through.

If you end your turn inside a Medium or smaller creature's space, that creature must make a Strength saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failed save, the creature is pushed 5 feet away from you into the nearest unoccupied space. On a successful save, you are pushed to the nearest unoccupied space instead.
### Level 10: Parasitic Consumption
When you make a Reckless Attack while your Rage is active, volatile energy arcs from your strike. The damage is also dealt to the enemy within 30 feet of you that is furthest away from you. If there is more than one enemy tied for that maximum distance, they divide the total damage dealt to your attack target equally among themselves.
### Level 14: Unstable Core
While your Rage is active, when you use your _Untamed Flight_ to fly through a creature's space for the first time on a turn, that creature takes Force damage equal to twice your Proficiency Bonus plus your Strength modifier.
