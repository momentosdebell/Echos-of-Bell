---
Name: Lumos
Spell Level: Cantrip (Level 0)
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 hour
Effect: You touch one object that is no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. Until the spell ends, the object sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and no dim light. The light can be colored as you like. Completely covering the object with something opaque blocks the light.
---
