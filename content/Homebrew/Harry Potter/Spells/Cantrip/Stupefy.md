---
Name: Stupefy
Spell Level: Cantrip
School: Enchantment
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 round
Attack Save: Constitution Save
Effect: You fire a jet of red light at a creature within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be dazed by the stunning force until the end of its next turn. While dazed, the target loses its reaction and can take either an action or a bonus action on its turn, but not both.

Stuns a target with a red flash, rendering them temporarily incapacitated.
