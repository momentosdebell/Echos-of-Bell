---
Name: Accio
Spell Level: Cantrip
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Higher Levels: The maximum weight of the object increases on character level to 20 lbs at 5th level, 30 lbs at 11th level, and 50 lbs at 17th level.
Effect:
  - You name or clearly picture an unattended object within range that is not being held, worn, or carried by a creature. If the target object weighs 10 lbs or less, it flies through the air directly into your open hand or lands at your feet. The object travels around soft obstacles and open corners. If its path is completely blocked by a solid barrier (such as a locked door or wall), it drops to the floor upon hitting the barrier.
---
