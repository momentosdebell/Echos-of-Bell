---
Name: Minor Conjurations
Spell Level: Cantrip
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Effect:
  - "Finestra: You instantly shatter a small, non-magical glass object within range."
  - "Periculum: You shoot a bright red distress flare or a shower of sparks high into the air."
  - "Orchideous: You conjure a temporary bouquet of flowers or a small flock of paper birds at the tip of your wand."
  - "Alarte Ascendare: You launch a small, lightweight object (weighing up to 5 lbs) up to 10 feet into the air."
Tags:
  - Spell
  - Homebrew
---
**Alarte Ascendare**
Shoots a target or small object high into the air.

**Periculum**
Launches a bright red distress flare high into the sky.

**Orchideous**
Conjures a bouquet of flowers or a small flock of birds from the wand.

**Finestra**
Instantly shatters glass surfaces into tiny fragments.
