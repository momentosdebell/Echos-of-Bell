---
Name: Tarantallegra
Spell Level: 1st Level
School: Enchantment
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
Effect: A creature you can see within range must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the target's legs begin to dance uncontrollably for the duration. While dancing, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls and Dexterity saving throws, and moving costs it double movement (disadvantage on Acrobatics/Athletics checks). At the end of each of its turns, the target can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.
---
