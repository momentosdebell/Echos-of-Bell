---
Name: Expelliarmus
Spell Level: 1st
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Damage: 2d6
Damage Type: Force
Higher Levels: When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the force damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
Effect: You release a jet of crimson light toward a creature holding a wand, weapon, or other held object. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the target takes 2d6 Force damage and the held object is violently disarmed, flying up to 20 feet away in a direction of your choice. If the object lands within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to make a DC 12 Dexterity check to catch it mid-air. On a successful save, the target takes half damage and holds onto the object.
