---
Name: Crucio
Spell Level: 1st
School: Necromancy
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Material: None
Duration: 1 minute
Concentration: true
Ritual: false
Attack Save: 5 Wisdom Save
Damage: 4d8
Damage Type: Psychic
Higher Levels: When cast using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, you bypass the backfire mechanic entirely.
Effect: You attempt to unleash the Torture Curse, channeling pure sadism into a target within range. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against DC 5. If the target's total saving throw result is 5 or higher, the dark magic violently rebounds off your weak intent, dealing 4d8 Psychic damage to you instead, knocking you prone from agonizing shock, and stunning you for 1 round. If the target's total saving throw result is lower than 5, the curse locks onto the target's nerves with sickening precision, dealing 4d8 Psychic damage on the first round and afflicting it with ongoing torture while you maintain concentration; at the start of each of its turns, the target takes 4d8 Psychic damage and must make a Wisdom saving throw where a failure means the pain is too intense and the target can only move without taking actions, bonus actions, or reactions that turn, lasting until your concentration breaks or the target is freed by a Remove Curse spell or similar magic.
