---
Name: Imperio
Spell Level: 1st
School: Enchantment
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Intelligence Save
Higher Levels: When cast using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, you bypass the backfire mechanic entirely. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw against your normal Spell Save DC.
Effect: You attempt to subtly bend a target's mind to your absolute will, instilling a dreamlike state of peaceful obedience. The target makes an Intelligence saving throw against your Intelligence save DC 5. If the target's total saving throw result is 5 or higher, your mental reach violently snaps back and shatters your composure, instantly inflicting 3 levels of Exhaustion and the Frightened condition toward the target; the Frightened condition is permanent and can only be cured by a Remove Curse, Greater Restoration, or similar magic. If the target's total saving throw result is lower than 5, the curse takes complete hold of its mind; the target is charmed by you and telepathically obeys all your verbal or mental commands without hesitation, performing social tasks, revealing secrets, or acting on your behalf as if it were its own peaceful desire, lasting until your concentration breaks or the target is freed by a Remove Curse spell or similar magic.
