---
Name: Petrificus Totalus
Spell Level: 1st Level
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Constitution Save
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The targets must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.
Effect:
  - A creature you can see within range must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target's body freezes completely rigid and its jaws lock tight. The target is incapacitated, its speed becomes 0, and it is unable to move, speak, or cast spells with Verbal components for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.
