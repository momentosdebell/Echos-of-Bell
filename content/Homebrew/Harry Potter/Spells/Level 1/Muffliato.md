---
Name: Muffliato
Spell Level: 1st Level
School: Illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 hour
Concentration: true
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radius of the effect increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 1st.
Effect: You create a 15-foot-radius sphere of buzzing sound centered on a point within range. Anyone outside the sphere hears only an unidentifiable, low buzzing noise when listening to conversations taking place inside the area. The spell does not block loud noises such as combat or shouting, but completely obscures spoken words and whispers.
