---
Name: Engorgio & Reducio
Spell Level: 1st
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S
Material: None
Duration: 1 hour
Concentration: true
Ritual: true
Effect:
  - "You choose one non-magical object within range that is not being worn or carried by an unwilling creature, altering its physical dimensions for the duration. When casting this spell, choose one of the following effects: Engorgio increases the object's size category by two steps, quadrupling its height, width, and length and multiplying its weight by eight; or Reducio reduces the object's size category by two steps, shrinking its dimensions to one-quarter of its original size and reducing its weight to one-eighth, making large or heavy items easily portable. If an enlarged object lacks space to expand, it grows to the maximum size the space permits and stops."
---
