---
Name: Protego Totalum
Spell Level: 5th Level
School: Abjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (15-foot radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the radius increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 5th.
Effect:
  - You conjure a shimmering, hemispherical barrier of protective energy in a 15-foot radius around you. Until the spell ends, you and any allies within the area gain a +4 bonus to AC and advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that originate from outside the barrier. Additionally, creatures inside the barrier have resistance to damage from spells and magical effects originating from outside. Creatures and objects can pass freely through the barrier in either direction.
