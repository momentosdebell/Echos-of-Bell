---
Name: Protego Diabolica
Spell Level: 8th Level
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (30-foot radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Dexterity
Damage: Fire
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 9th leve, the damage increases by 2d10
Effect:
  - You create a ring of black or blue magical fire around yourself with a radius of up to 30 feet. The ring moves with you and lasts for the duration. When you cast the spell, you can designate any number of creatures you can see to be immune to its effects; the flames pass harmlessly around them. When a hostile creature enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must make a Dexterity saving throw. The creature takes 8d10 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If a creature is reduced to 0 hit points by this damage, its body is instantly consumed and turned to ash. As an action on your turn while maintaining concentration, you can command the flames to surge outward, forcing every hostile creature within the area to immediately make the saving throw against the spell's damage.
