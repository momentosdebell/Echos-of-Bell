---
Name: Bombarda
Spell Level: 3rd Level
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Material: ""
Duration: Instantaneous
Concentration: false
Ritual: false
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Damage: 6d6
Damage Type: Force
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher (Bombarda Maxima), the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd. Additionally, if cast using a 5th-level or higher spell slot, the explosion radius increases from 10 feet to 20 feet.
Effect:
  - You choose a target or space within range, creating a sudden, violent explosion. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 6d6 Force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. On a failure, a creature is also knocked Prone. Nonmagical objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried take double damage from the explosion, and mundane locks, barricades, or structures in the area are instantly shattered.
---
