---
Name: Bombarda
Spell Level: 3rd Level
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Damage: 6d6
Damage Type: Force
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd. Additionally, if cast using a 5th-level (Bombarda Maxima) or higher spell slot, the explosion radius increases from 10 feet to 20 feet.
Effect:
  - You choose a target or space within range, creating a sudden, violent explosion. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 6d6 Force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. On a failure, a creature is also knocked Prone. Nonmagical objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried take full damage from the explosion.
