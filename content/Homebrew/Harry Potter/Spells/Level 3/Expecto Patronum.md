---
Name: Expecto Patronum
Spell Level: 3rd Level
School: Abjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S, M (a happy memory)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Ritual: false
Attack Save: Wisdom Save
Damage: 3d8
Damage Type: Radiant
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 3rd. Additionally, when cast using a 4th-level or higher spell slot, you can choose to cast it to deliver a spoken message of up to 25 words to a creature you know on the same plane of existence instead of summoning a combat guardian.
Effect:
  - You summon a silvery, animalistic guardian spirit into an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. The spirit sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. For the duration, you can use a bonus action to move the spirit up to 30 feet. You can use your action to cause the spirit to emit a burst of radiant energy. Each creature within 10 feet of the spirit must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 3d8 Radiant damage and is pushed 10 feet away from the spirit. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't pushed. Fiends and Undead have disadvantage on this saving throw, while creatures that are neither Fiends nor Undead take only half damage on a failed save (and no damage on a successful save).
