---
Name: Sectumsempra
Spell Level: 7th Level
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Material: ""
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Ritual: false
Attack Save: Dexterity
Damage: Slashing
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the initial damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 7th.
Effect:
  - You slash the air toward a creature you can see within range, unleashing invisible, razor-sharp blades of dark magic. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 8d8 slashing damage and begins deeply bleeding, taking 2d8 slashing damage at the start of each of its turns. On a success, the target takes half as much damage and does not bleed. A bleeding creature (or an adjacent ally) can use an action to make a Wisdom (Medicine) check against your spell save DC to staunch the wound, ending the ongoing damage effect.
---
