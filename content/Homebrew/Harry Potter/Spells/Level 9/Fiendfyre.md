---
Name: Fiendfyre
Spell Level: 9th Level
School: Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 120 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Dexterity
Damage: Fire
Effect:
  - You conjure a massive, roaring spirit of cursed flame that takes the form of a fiery beast, such as a dragon, serpent, or chimera, in an unoccupied space within range. The fire spirit occupies a 20-foot cube. When you cast the spell, and as a bonus action on each of your turns thereafter, you can mentally command the spirit to move up to 60 feet and sweep through creatures in its path. Each creature in or within 10 feet of the spirit's path must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 12d10 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The cursed flames ignore fire resistance and treat fire immunity as resistance. If your concentration is broken before the spell ends, the fire spirit does not disappear; instead, it loses all control and moves toward the nearest living creature on each of its turns, attacking indiscriminately until the duration expires.
---
