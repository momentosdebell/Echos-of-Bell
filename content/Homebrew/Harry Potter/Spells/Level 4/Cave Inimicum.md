---
Name: Cave Inimicum
Spell Level: 4th Level
School: Abjuration
Casting Time: 1 minute
Range: Self (30-foot radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: 8 hours
Ritual: true
Attack Save: Wisdom Save
Damage: None
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the radius of the protected area increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 4th.
Effect:
  - You trace an invisible boundary in up to a 30-foot radius around yourself, anchoring it to the ground for the duration. The area inside is completely invisible and silent from the outside, blocking all light, sound, and scent from escaping. Any creature that approaches or attempts to enter the area must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature feels a strong urge to turn away and avoid the area, completely unaware of the protected zone. On a success, the creature resists the effect and can freely enter. Regardless of whether a creature succeeds or fails, you are mentally alerted the moment any creature crosses the boundary, which instantly awakens you if you are asleep.
