---
Name: Appare Vestigium
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Divination
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S,
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
Concentration: true
Ritual: true
Attack Save: None
Damage: None
Damage Type: None
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the duration increases by 10 minutes and you can look back an additional 8 hours into the past for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect: For the duration, your eyes glow faintly as you perceive magical traces, footprints, and lingering energy signatures left behind by creatures or spells within 30 feet of you. You can see glowing ghostly outlines showing where creatures walked, what spells were cast, and what objects were used within the last 24 hours. The intensity and color of the traces provide clues to the nature of the magic or creature that left them.
