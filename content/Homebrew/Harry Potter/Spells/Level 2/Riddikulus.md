---
Name: Riddikulus
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Illusion
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Duration: 1 round
Concentration: false
Attack Save: Wisdom Save
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect: Target a creature you can see within range. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, its appearance, armor, or weapon visually transforms into something ridiculous until the start of your next turn. During this time, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls and Charisma (Intimidation) checks, and cannot impose the Frightened condition on any creature. Additionally, any creature within range that is Frightened by the target immediately ends the Frightened condition on itself.
