---
Name: Incarcerous
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Conjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Damage: 3d6
Damage Type: Bludgeoning
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the target stays in the air for 1 additional round and the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect:
  - Heavy magical ropes burst forth, binding a creature you can see within range and hoisting it 5 feet into the air. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the target is suspended mid-air and its speed becomes 0, though it can still take actions and bonus actions. At the start of each of its turns, the target (or an ally within 5 feet) can make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC to break free early, safely landing on its feet. If the target does not break free before the duration in the air ends, the ropes release, slamming the target to the ground where it takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and falls prone.
---
