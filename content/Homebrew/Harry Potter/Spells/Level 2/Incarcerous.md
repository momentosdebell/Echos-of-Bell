---
Name: Incarcerous
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Conjuration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Attack Save: Dexterity Save
Damage: 3d6
Damage Type: Bludgeoning
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect:
  - Heavy magical ropes burst forth, binding a creature you can see within range and hoisting it 5 feet into the air. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the target is restrained mid-air for 1 round. It cannot move, but can still take actions, bonus actions, reaction and speak. At the start of its next turn, the spell slams the target back down to the ground, dealing 3d6 bludgeoning damage. Once on the ground, the target remains restrained by the ropes. As an action on its turn, the target (or a creature within 5 feet) can make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC to break free and end the spell.
---
