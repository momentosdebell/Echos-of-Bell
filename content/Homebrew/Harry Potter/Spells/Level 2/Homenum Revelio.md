---
Name: Homenum Revelio
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Divination
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (60-foot radius)
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Ritual: true
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radius of the effect increases by 30 feet for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect: You send out a subtle, undetectable wave of divination magic. You instantly sense the presence, exact location, and total number of humanoids within a 60-foot radius of you. This spell reveals humanoids that are hidden, invisible, or disguised, as well as those behind total cover (such as behind walls or doors). It does not reveal non-humanoid creatures (such as beasts, undead, or fiends).
---
