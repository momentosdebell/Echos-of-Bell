---
Name: Diffindo
Spell Level: 2nd Level
School: Transmutation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Attack Save: Ranged Spell Attack
Damage: 3d10
Damage Type: Slashing
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.
Effect: A beam of precise, cutting magical force streaks toward a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 3d10 slashing damage. Alternatively, you can target a nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried, instantly severing or cutting through a single nonmagical object up to 1 foot thick (such as a rope, heavy chain, wooden beam, or padlock).
