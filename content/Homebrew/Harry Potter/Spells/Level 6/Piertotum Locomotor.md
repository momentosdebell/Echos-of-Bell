---
Name: Piertotum Locomotor
Spell Level: 6th Level
School: Transfiguration
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Concentration: true
Damage: Bludgeoning / Slashing
Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, you can animate one additional Medium construct (or one Large construct for every two slot levels above 6th).
Effect:
  - You command the environment to rise and defend you, animating either up to two Large or up to four Medium objects within range, such as statues or unworn suits of armor. If no suitable objects are present, the magic draws upon surrounding materials like stones, debris, or furniture to form the constructs. Animated constructs act on your initiative and obey your mental commands. As a bonus action, you can command any or all construct to move and make one melee attack. Medium constructs (+4 to hit, reach 5 ft) deal 2d6 + 1 bludgeoning or slashing damage. Large constructs (+6 to hit, reach 5 ft) deal 2d10 + 2 bludgeoning or slashing damage.
