## 📜 Overview
Far below the ground, where the sun never shines and people above never look, lies a secret world called **Bram’vorn**.

Above this hidden forest, the high stone ceiling is filled with shining crystals. They give the land the light it needs and create a slow day and night. One full day and night here lasts thirty hours, twenty hours of bright crystal day, and ten hours of quiet, dim night.

When you walk down through the dark earth and open your eyes, you do not find cold, empty stone. You find a giant, breathing forest. Huge ancient trees reach up toward the crystal roof. Soft lights in blue, purple, gold, and pale green float and glow from mushrooms, plants, and shining stones. Clear rivers move slowly through the trees, carrying mist and soft light past mossy stones and hanging roots.

Among the giant trees and roots sit towns like [[Thalasor]], a city filed with Drows, Kobolds some Firbolgs aswell as Mychonids but mainly and the birthplace of House- Elves. Long ago, their people were elves, before many generations in this deep forest slowly shaped them into small, quick people of the shadows. To them, Bram’vorn is not a dark cave hiding from the world. It is their home, warm, old, and full of living magic and stories waiting to be told.
