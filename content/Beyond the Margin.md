## 📜 Overview
A cramped, dusty bookshop wedged into a damp alleyway in [[Rivergold (Selgaunt)]], where the air smells of old paper, salt-cured leather, and cheap pipe weed. Run by a cynical, aging former cartographer who specializes in "unlicensed" maritime charts, old ship logs, and banned political pamphlets. For the right price, and if you know how to talk to the owner, it's one of the best spots to fence stolen intelligence, track down missing cargo manifests, or lay low when the city guards get too curious.
