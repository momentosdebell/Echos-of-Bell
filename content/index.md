## Rule of Cool
This is my thoughts on some table rules.

• DM's Word is Law: You can definitely try to plead your case or argue your point to the DM, but at the end of the day, the DM's call stands.

• Story Over Rules: All these rules are fun until they aren't. If a strict reading of the mechanics gets in the way of a great story or a cool cinematic moment, narrative flow wins every single time.

• Flanking: Love it for both players and DMs. Good positioning should always pay off, making the battlefield feel alive and tactical.

• Symmetry: If a player can cast a specific spell, use a mechanic, or pull off a clever trick, the DM gets to use it too. What's good for the goose is good for the dragon.

• Secret Death Saves: Rolling them privately to the DM. It keeps the real panic and tension alive.

• Crit Fails (Fumbles): Fully on board with the chaos. When a natural one goes spectacularly wrong, it's just too funny not to embrace.

• Encumberance (Coins Excluded): Yes to tracking gear and heavy equipment, but keeping pocketfuls of gold out of the math please, well everything has its limits though.

• Shared Inspiration: Both players and the DM can hand out inspiration whenever someone does something genuinely epic, cinematic, or hilarious.

• Potions: Bonus action to chug your own healing potion, but a full action to force one down a teammate's throat.

• Secret Social Rolls: Persuasion and deception rolls against other party members are rolled privately to the DM, and you keep it a secret from your fellow players which skill you actually used.
