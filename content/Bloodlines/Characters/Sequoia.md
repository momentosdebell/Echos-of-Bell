## 📜 Overview

> Sequoia is a living, tree-like Warforged druid born in 1471 through the combined arcane and primal magic of the renowned Loxodon wizard **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]** and the Firbolg archdruid **[[Flow Windgust]]**. Originally begun as a workshop project when Gaganesis needed an assistant, Sequoia became far more than intended once Flow lent his druidic craft to the work. Raised in **[[Silvergrove City (Silverymoon)]]** as Gaganesis's assistant, friend, and child, Sequoia blends traits of both parents while still figuring out who they truly are. Now on an open-ended pilgrimage encouraged by their mother, they travel the world to learn, grow, and understand themselves before one day taking over **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]**.

## 🎭 Personality

> Sequoia is an unhurried blend of both parents—carrying Flow’s calm, unbothered steadiness alongside Gaganesis’s sense of fairness, practical order, and quiet strength—even if they have not quite figured themselves out yet. Observant and earnest, they tend to wear their heart plainly on their branches, where shifting leaves and seasonal fruit often give away what words do not.

## 🏠 Background

**Origin** Sequoia was created in 1471 when **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]**, who loved crafting and needed an assistant for her shop, began building a construct. After corresponding with **[[Flow Windgust]]**, by then an archdruid, Flow lent his primal magic to her arcane work—bringing Sequoia to life as something far more living and complex than either had planned.

**Upbringing** Sequoia grew up in **[[Silvergrove City (Silverymoon)]]** alongside **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]**, working as her assistant at **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]** while sharing a close bond with her as both friend and child. Though they know **[[Flow Windgust]]** well and have spoken with him several times, their bond is more that of friendly acquaintances than parent and child.

**Culture** Shaped primarily by life in **[[Silvergrove City (Silverymoon)]]** and the daily rhythm of **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]**, Sequoia grew up surrounded by high arcane craft, structured city life, and fair-minded practicality, balanced against the quiet, instinctive pull of the natural magic woven into their ironwood body.

**Education** Most of Sequoia's practical education came directly from assisting **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]** in her shop—learning the basics of running a business, handling customers, and navigating an arcane environment—while their druidic magic developed naturally from the primal core Flow helped awaken.

**Turning Point** There was no sudden tragedy that drove Sequoia from home. Seeing Sequoia not as a creation but truly as her child, **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]** encouraged them to set out into the world to explore, learn everything they could, and discover who they are—with the long-term hope that Sequoia will one day take over **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]** when she is ready to step back.

**Consequences**

- **Living tell:** Because their canopy grows fruit whose flavor shifts with their emotions, hiding joy, grief, or anger is nearly impossible around anyone paying attention.
    
- **Out of place indoors:** While raised in a city shop, their towering, living-wood body leaves trails of moss and blossoms behind and stands out sharply in sterile, enclosed rooms.
    
- **Still finding their footing:** Caught between arcane upbringing, primal nature, and two larger-than-life parents, Sequoia is still defining their own identity outside of being an assistant.
    

**Affiliations**

- **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]:** Gaganesis’s shop in **[[Silvergrove City (Silverymoon)]]**, where Sequoia worked as an assistant and which they are meant to inherit one day.
    
- **[[Gaganesis Thorne]]:** Their mother, mentor, and closest tie in the world.
    
- **[[Flow Windgust]] & the Windgust family:** Their creator-father and his family across the continent in **[[Ever Peak (Sundabar)]]**.
    

**Motivations**

- **Self-discovery:** Understand who they are on their own terms, separate from their parents' legacies and their role as an assistant.
    
- **Learn from the world:** Gather experience, wisdom, and practical knowledge during their open-ended pilgrimage.
    
- **Prepare for the future:** Grow capable enough to eventually take over **[[Dynamic Office Of Reality]]** when Gaganesis steps down.
    

**Secrets**

- **Uncertain identity:** Beneath their calm, steady exterior, Sequoia quietly admits they do not yet know who they really are or where they truly belong.
    
- **Famous lineage:** They rarely advertise that their mother is one of the realm's most renowned wizards or that their co-creator is a famous archdruid.
    

**Bridge** Sequoia steps into the campaign mid-pilgrimage—traveling without a fixed destination, open to new companions and experiences, and ready to lend their steady presence and living nature magic wherever the road leads.

## 💪 Abilities

#### Verdant Footsteps

Sequoia leaves no ordinary tracks behind. Wherever they walk, a small, vibrant trail of flora sprouts in their wake instead of footprints. The growth adapts instantly to their current environment: wild clover and moss might push through the cracks of city cobblestones, glowing fungi might sprout on damp cave floors, or elegant, manicured blossoms might emerge upon the polished hardwood of a noble’s estate.

#### Environmental Camouflage

As a creature of living wood and deep magic, Sequoia’s ability to blend into their surroundings is entirely dictated by nature's presence. The Dungeon Master has the final say on whether the current location can plausibly harbor roots or foliage.

- **Natural Affinity (+5 Stealth):** They gain a +5 bonus to Stealth checks in environments where trees, dense vegetation, or ancient roots naturally belong, such as deep forests, overgrown ruins, or root-riddled caverns.
    
- **Unnatural Presence (-5 Stealth):** Conversely, they suffer a -5 penalty to Stealth checks in sterile or enclosed spaces where a giant tree-man is jarringly out of place, such as a noble's pristine bedroom or a stone treasury.
    

#### Emotional Harvest

Sequoia naturally grows unique fruits from their canopy, the flavor of which is entirely tethered to their current emotional state. Those who partake can instantly taste their true feelings:

- **Joy & Happiness:** The fruits become incredibly sweet, rich, and bursting with vibrant flavor.
    
- **Grief & Sadness:** The flesh of the fruit turns intensely bitter, sharp, and hard to swallow.
    
- **Anger & Stress:** The fruit might taste spicy, or leave a burning sensation on the tongue.
    

#### Seasonal Metamorphosis

Sequoia’s physical form shifts dynamically to reflect the surrounding ambient temperature, acting as a living calendar: ![[Sequoia-Alt.png]]

- **High Temperatures:** Their canopy thickens with lush, vibrant green leaves, trailing vines, and a heavy abundance of fruits.
    
- **Temperate Climates:** Their foliage thins slightly, making way for an explosion of colorful flowers and blooming buds.
    
- **Cooling Climates:** Their leaves undergo a breathtaking transition, changing into brilliant shades of fiery orange, deep yellow, and rich red.
    
- **Freezing Temperatures:** Their leaves wither and fall away completely. Their exposed wood becomes hardened and dormant, their skin functioning and looking exactly like an ordinary, wintering tree.
    

#### Innate Spell Alterations

All of Sequoia's magic flows directly from within their own biological and mechanical core. Rather than invoking external forces, their spells are physical, nature-based extensions of their living ironwood body.

- **Flame Blade, now Thorn Fists:** Instead of conjuring a blazing blade of fire, Sequoia channels primal energy into their hands. Their fists erupt with massive, jagged, rose-like thorns. When they strike, these heavy thorns deliver brutal piercing trauma infused with a toxic sap, dealing Poison damage instead of the standard fire damage.
    
- **Ice Knife, now Resin Shard:** Sequoia hardens their natural sap into a dense, crystallized projectile. They hurl a heavy clump of solid resin at a target, dealing Bludgeoning damage on impact instead of piercing. Immediately after impact, the volatile resin ball shatters and explodes violently, sending sharp wood-and-resin shrapnel flying outward to deal Piercing damage to all nearby targets instead of cold damage.
    
- **Chill Touch, now Venomous Root:** Instead of conjuring a ghostly hand, Sequoia unleashes a thorny vine. It whips out directly from their wrist toward the target, latching on with a toxic grip that deals Poison damage and prevents the target from regaining hit points until the start of Sequoia's next turn.
