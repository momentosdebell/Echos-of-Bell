## ​📜 Overview 
Mavis carries the quiet weight of a long, unsettled life shaped by movement, caution, and hard-won self-reliance. Raised as an only child in [[Thalasor]] by Nessa and Corin, she grew up surrounded by everyday magic, practical order, dry humor, and clear boundaries before leaving her home behind in 1472. Across decades of couriering, night watch, bitter prejudice when mistaken for a goblin while trying to settle, and eventually joining [[Old-Hag]] in 1535, she has kept a calm, guarded front that hides a sharp streak of mischief and a steady nerve when trouble hits.

## 🎭Personality  
Observant, cautious, and steady under pressure, with a dry wit and a calm outer edge that hides a sharp streak of mischief. She rarely accepts "certain truths" without testing them, refuses to let anyone push her around, and keeps her guard up until trust is earned.

## 🏠Background 

   **Origin** 
Born in 1409 in [[Thalasor]] within [[Bram’vorn]], a vast subterranean forest civilization hidden from the surface world on the Material Plane. Although House-Elves are regarded today as a goblin subrace, Nook’s people began as elves whose generations-long adaptation to subterranean forests, roots, and caves shaped them into small, nimble survivors, leaving an ancient heritage that surface dwellers rarely recognize or understand.

   **Upbringing** 
Growing up as an only child on the stepped stone terraces of [[Thalasor]], Mavis was raised by two mothers, Nessa and Corin, in a busy city of [[Bram’vorn]]. They were an ordinary, middle-of-the-road household, neither rich nor poor, with no fancy airs, with Nessa working as a supply steward and Corin as a records sorter across the rock levels. Their home balanced genuine warmth and a strong hunger to know things, shaped by a tone that was fairly stiff and professional day to day, yet broken up by flashes of spontaneity and dry humor. Magic was completely ordinary in their house, just part of daily life, nothing strange or special, while Nessa and Corin taught Mavis loyalty, taking care of herself, not taking crap from anyone, and remembering that you can never be certain of what you think you know because there is always more to learn and new proof waiting to be found. Quiet and observant, with a cool outward edge, thick skin, and a streak for mischief she rarely shows on the surface.

   **Turning Point** 
Everything changed when a brood of purple worms broke up through the rock levels of the city, tearing it apart from below. Stepped stone terraces collapsed, tunnels gave way, and panic swept through the streets as people were forced to flee for their lives. Nessa and Corin did not make it out, leaving Mavis with the devastating loss of both mothers while she survived the chaos by reading the shifting rock, slipping through narrow passages, and keeping her head in the middle of a frantic flight. Leaving [[Thalasor]] behind turned her quiet observation and self-reliance from childhood habits into survival instincts, carrying what she learned at home into a hard, uncertain road ahead.

   **Consequences** 
Surviving the collapse of [[Thalasor]] around the age of sixty and losing Nessa and Corin left Mavis with decades of quiet weight to carry, including a lingering edge of claustrophobia in tight spaces or beneath heavy stone where the memory of falling rock still stays with her. She still struggles to put down roots or call anywhere new a true home, automatically checks overhead cover, listens for subtle shifts or vibrations, keeps her own footing rather than letting anyone push her around, and holds back from trusting a place or group until she has seen it hold up over time. More than seventy years of moving on without a settled home reinforced her belief that no situation is ever completely certain, keeping her slow to open up at first while calm, observant, and steady when things go sideways.
