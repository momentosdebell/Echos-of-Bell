## 📜 Overview
Araevin Teshurr is a young Sun Elf noble who desperately wishes he wasn't a Sorcerer. Caught between his father’s traditional lineage (House Teshurr) and his mother’s toxic, blood-soaked sorcerer dynasty, where a brutal tradition dictates fatal magical duels against fathers, he views his magic as a curse. Terrified of the chaotic power surging within him, he actively suppresses it, refusing to embrace his sorcery and relying instead on strict discipline, swordplay, and his father's academic legacy.

## 🎭 Personality
Hides his innate sorcery with deep resentment, refusing to identify as a Sorcerer and viewing his powers as a shameful infection.

Constantly keeps his feelings in check, knowing that fear, stress, or adrenaline can instantly shatter his control and unleash chaotic magic.
## 🏰 Background
#### Origin
Born into a high-stakes union between the noble traditions of House Teshurr on his fathers side, and a powerful, deeply aristocratic sorcerer dynasty on his mother's side.
#### Upbringing
Raised under immense psychological pressure. On his mother's side, sorcery is treated as an absolute birthright tied to strict political marriages and a horrific generational tradition: a mandatory magical duel to the death between kin. 
#### Education
Studied at the Tower of Reilloch, trying to bury his sorcerer blood under layers of history, theory, and rigorous martial discipline.
#### Affiliations
- House Teshurr (Father's side / Noble lineage)
- House [[Everfall]] (Mother's side / Feared sorcerer bloodline)
#### Motivations
To permanently purge or lock away his sorcery, survive the expectations of his bloodline, and prove his worth through the blade rather than raw innate magic.
#### Secrets
He lives in constant terror that his emotional control will snap, and that he will either lose control in a public surge.

## 💪 Abilities
- Wild Magic triggers from when he experiences intense emotions, fear, or, most dangerously, when he successfully strikes an enemy with his sword in combat.
- Altered Starlight Eyes: A permanent mark from his heritage, eyes. glowing with a faint amber light.
## ⚔️ Equipment
- Moonrill (+1 Longsword). His primary anchor and Arcane Focus. He relies on the physical weight and celestial light (Daylight, 1/day) of the blade rather than spellcasting.
- Tower of Reilloch Arcane Robes: Elegant, midnight-blue academic wear.
- House Teshurr Signet Ring: Gold family crest ring.
