## 📜 Overview
**Agnes Ekblad** is a tall, resilient human innkeeper in **[[Ever Peak (Sundabar)]]** and longtime proprietor of **[[The Shelf]]**. Inheriting her notable stature and grit from her Goliath grandmother, she spent decades transforming a century-old hillside home alongside her husband, **[[Gustav Ekblad]]** ("GusGus"). Warm, lively, and known to every guest by name, Agnes turned the tavern she inherited from her mother, **[[Bromhilda Winterfrost]]**, into a living time capsule of stories, comfort, and community.

## 🎭 Personality
 Bubbly, warm, and tough-minded, Agnes combines a playful spark and welcoming hospitality with the stubborn grit inherited from her grandmother. She loves reminiscing about her youth and adventures with **[[Gustav Ekblad]]**, greets every guest by name, and stays steady through hard times.

## 🏠 Background
**Origin** Agnes is a human whose tall build, physical resilience, and grit trace back to her Goliath grandmother.

**Upbringing** Agnes grew up in the hillside building that became **[[The Shelf]]** alongside her sister, **[[Aurora Ekblad|Aurora]]**, after their mother, **[[Bromhilda Winterfrost]]**, bought the house from the Moliner family. Agnes began helping out with the tavern around ten years old, learning early on how to keep a gathering place welcoming through hard work and care before inheriting the property from her mother.

**Culture** Rooted in the tavern and village life of **[[Ever Peak (Sundabar)]]**, shaped by local history, and decades of shared stories around the hearth.

**Education** Practical, hands-on experience in hospitality, tavern operations, and keeping **[[The Shelf]]** afloat through lean years and shifting times.


**Consequences**
- **A living legacy:** Decades of work by Agnes and GusGus, adding the basement and balcony, updating rooms, and collecting souvenirs, murals, and guestbooks, turned the building into a landmark for the village.

**Affiliations**
- **[[The Shelf]]:** Longtime host and proprietor in **[[Ever Peak (Sundabar)]]**, supported for two months each year by apprentices **Pil** and **Rut** (the Rosenkrans sisters, in their thirties).
- **Ekblad / Winterfrost / Windgust Family:** Married to **[[Gustav Ekblad]]**, connecting her to her "child" **[[Princess Wiggles]]**, her mother **[[Bromhilda Winterfrost]]**, her sister **[[Aurora Ekblad|Aurora]]**, and her in-laws **[[Flow Windgust]]**, **[[Safir Windgust]]**, **[[Gullvi Nimue]]**, and **[[Sequoia]]**.
    

**Motivations**
- **Care for her family:** Look after her "child", **[[Princess Wiggles]]**, her sister **[[Aurora Ekblad|Aurora]]**, and honor her bond with **[[Gustav Ekblad]]**.
- **Keep [[The Shelf]] alive:** Ensure the tavern remains a warm sanctuary for locals and travelers under new care.
    
## 💪 Abilities
**Path of the Beast (Flavor):** Agnes fights as a Path of the Beast Barbarian, though her rage manifests through fierce, animalistic behavior rather than monstrous physical mutations.

- **Hair-Whip ("Tail"):** Instead of growing a beastly tail, Agnes whips and lashes out with her own hair.
- **Natural Nails ("Claws"):** She always wears ordinary, everyday nails rather than sprouting claws, tearing into foes through sheer ferocity and strength.

## 💞 Relationships
- **[[Gustav Ekblad]] ("GusGus") (Husband):** Her husband, former craftsman, and occasional tavern musician—also quietly known in whispers as "Vit Hövdingen" or "Hövdingen" (tied to the white-tailed sea eagle). His disappearance left Agnes devastated.
- **[[Princess Wiggles]] ("Child"):** Her dog.
- **[[Aurora Ekblad|Aurora]] (Sister):** Agnes’s sister.
- **[[Bromhilda Winterfrost]] (Mother):** Agnes and Aurora’s mother, who purchased the house from the Moliner family, began transforming it, and later left it to Agnes.
- **Pil & Rut (Rosenkrans Sisters):** Apprentices in their thirties who run **[[The Shelf]]**'s daily operations during the two months each year when Agnes and Gustav travel, hike, or visit old friends.
- **[[Flow Windgust]], [[Safir Windgust]], [[Gullvi Nimue]] & [[Sequoia]]:** Extended family through Gustav.
    

## 🗓️ Events
- **Early years at the house:** Grew up in the building after her mother, **[[Bromhilda Winterfrost]]**, bought it from the original builders (the Moliner family), starting work there around age ten before eventually inheriting it.
- **Built up [[The Shelf]]:** Transformed the home into **[[The Shelf]]** alongside **[[Gustav Ekblad]]**, adding the basement and balcony, updating rooms, and hosting the tavern six days a week for ten months a year while taking two months annually to travel, hike, or visit old friends.
- **Supported by royal tax relief:** The tavern weathered difficult periods in part thanks to royal tax relief.    

## 🍿 Trivia

- **[[The Shelf]]** gets its name from a young Agnes's plump breasts, a point of pride and local legend.    
- Much of the interior comes from Agnes and **[[Gustav Ekblad]]**'s travels, including an old chair that is actually a harmless Mimic content to just be part of their lives.
- The tavern features handmade crocheted tablecloths, a central open fireplace, worn leather armchairs, antique mirrors, murals, portraits, guestbooks, and battle maps across the first floor, second floor, and basement.
- **[[The Shelf]]** has historically benefited from royal tax relief during hard times.
