## 📜 Overview
 **Sir Lucian Yllaris Everfall** is a young elven prince, still little more than an adolescent by elven standards, and the son of King **[[Theodemar Yllaris Everfall]]** and Queen **[[Lilliana Yllaris Everfall]]**. Convinced he is destined to be a legendary warrior, he parades around in expensive gear and throws gold around with zero grasp of what anything actually costs. In truth, he is terrible with a sword, easily flattered and taken advantage of by opportunists, and completely unaware, or unwilling to accept, that his real potential lies elseware.

## 🎭 Personality
Arrogant, spoiled, and brimming with unearned confidence, Lucian genuinely believes he is the best at everything he tries. He behaves like a seasoned warrior, always ready with an excuse whenever his swings miss or his plans go sideways. Generous to a fault simply because money means nothing to him, he is loud, easily manipulated by anyone willing to stroke his ego, and desperate to prove himself, even as the constant attention and exploitation from strangers wear on him.

## 🏠 Background
**Origin** Born into the royal **Yllaris, Everfall** family as the son of King **[[Theodemar Yllaris Everfall]]** and Queen **[[Lilliana Yllaris Everfall]]**, and younger brother to **[[Lily Everfall]]**.

**Upbringing** Raised in royal luxury with every whim indulged, leaving him with no concept of the value of money or the realities of life outside the palace walls.

**Consequences**
- **A walking target:** Because he is famous, wealthy, and reckless with coin, merchants, scammers, and hangers-on constantly flock to him, flatter him, and exploit his naivety.
- **Out of his depth:** Insisting on acting like a frontline warrior despite lacking the skill puts him, and anyone traveling with him, in constant danger.
    
**Affiliations**
- **The Royal House:** Royal family by birth.

**Motivations**
- **Prove himself as a "great warrior":** Earn glory and respect on his own terms rather than just being known as the spoiled prince.
- **Avoid being controlled:** Escape the pressure, expectations, and people trying to use him for his name and coin.
    

**Secrets**
- Despite carrying himself like a master-at-arms, he is genuinely awful at martial combat, and his actual aptitude lies in wizardry, a path he has yet to embrace.

## ⚔️ Equipment
- **Overpriced, Flashy Gear:** Carries expensive, ornate weapons and gear bought with royal gold without knowing their true worth or whether he can even use them properly.
- **Excessive Coin:** Carries far more gold than he knows how to manage, spending lavishly on mundane things.

## 🗓️ Events
- **Left the royal court:** Ventured out into the world at age 44 determined to prove himself as a mighty warrior.
